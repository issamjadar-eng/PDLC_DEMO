# GL-SOP-QM-003 — Training and Competence

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-QM-003
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Ensure that personnel performing work affecting product quality are competent on the basis of appropriate education, training, skills, and experience — per ISO 13485:2016 §6.2 and 21 CFR 820.25.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all GlobalLogic employees, contractors, and consultants performing QMS-governed activities, including design, software development, manufacturing, servicing, quality, regulatory, clinical, and post-market roles.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR / People Ops | Maintain personnel records; onboarding |
| Line Manager | Define role competence; identify training needs; approve training plans |
| Training Coordinator (Quality) | Maintain training matrix; track completion; coordinate training delivery |
| Individual | Complete assigned training; demonstrate competence |

## 4. Definitions

- **Competence** — Demonstrated ability to apply knowledge and skills.
- **Training** — The process by which knowledge or skill is imparted.
- **Role Training Matrix** — A matrix mapping roles to required QMS documents and trainings.

## 5. References

- ISO 13485:2016 §6.2
- 21 CFR 820.25
- EU MDR 2017/745 Art. 15 (Person Responsible for Regulatory Compliance)

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Competence Definition

For each role, the Line Manager documents:

- Education and/or experience baseline
- Required trainings (SOPs, WIs, product-specific, regulatory)
- Competence-verification method (read-and-sign, quiz, supervised execution, signed-off practical)

### 6.2 Role Training Matrix

The Training Coordinator maintains a matrix of `Role × Document/Training`. A new or revised SOP triggers a training-impact assessment (per GL-SOP-QM-001 §6.3) identifying which roles must be re-trained before the effective date.

### 6.3 Training Delivery

Training methods include:

- **Read-and-understand** — read the document, sign Training Record
- **Classroom / e-learning** — instructor-led or asynchronous with quiz
- **Practical / OJT** — supervised execution; trainer signs off after observed competence
- **Regulatory awareness** — annual refreshers (ISO 13485, risk, cybersecurity, GxP)

### 6.4 Records

Each training completion produces a **Training Record (GL-FORM-QM-003)** capturing:

- Name, role, date
- Document/training title and revision
- Method and outcome
- Trainer signature (if applicable)
- Trainee signature

### 6.5 Competence Gap / Failure

Personnel who fail to demonstrate competence shall not perform the affected activity until re-training and re-assessment. Repeated failures trigger a CAPA evaluation (GL-SOP-QM-005).

### 6.6 Awareness

All personnel are made aware of the relevance and importance of their activities and how they contribute to the achievement of quality objectives and the safety of patients and users.
