# GL-SOP-QM-002 — Management Review

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-QM-002
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech
**Supersedes:** None

---

## 1. Purpose

Define how Top Management reviews the GlobalLogic QMS to ensure continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness, in accordance with ISO 13485:2016 §5.6 and 21 CFR 820.20(c).

## 2. Scope

Applies to GlobalLogic MedTech business unit and any division whose products are covered by the GlobalLogic QMS.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Top Management | Attend review; authorize actions; provide resources |
| Management Representative (VP Quality) | Schedule, chair, distribute pre-read, capture minutes |
| Process Owners | Prepare and present inputs for their domain |
| Quality Engineering | Consolidate metrics and data trends |

## 4. Definitions

- **Management Review** — A planned, recurring evaluation of QMS performance by Top Management.
- **Process Owner** — The individual accountable for the effectiveness of a QMS process.

## 5. References

- ISO 13485:2016 §5.6 Management review
- 21 CFR 820.20 Management responsibility
- EU MDR 2017/745 Art. 10 General obligations of manufacturers

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Frequency

Management Review shall occur **at least quarterly**. Additional reviews may be triggered by:

- Critical field-safety issues
- Major regulatory inspection findings
- Significant changes in organization, products, or applicable regulations

### 6.2 Inputs (ISO 13485 §5.6.2)

Required inputs:

1. Feedback (customer, clinical, PMS) — from GL-SOP-PM-001
2. Complaint handling — from GL-SOP-PM-002
3. Regulatory reporting — from GL-SOP-PM-003
4. Monitoring and measurement of processes
5. Monitoring and measurement of product — V&V data
6. Audits — internal (GL-SOP-QM-004), external, supplier
7. Nonconforming product — from GL-SOP-SP-004
8. Data analysis — statistical trends, CAPA effectiveness
9. CAPA status — open / closed / overdue
10. Follow-up actions from previous management reviews
11. Changes that could affect the QMS — regulatory, organizational, technological
12. Recommendations for improvement
13. Applicable new or revised regulatory requirements

### 6.3 Outputs (ISO 13485 §5.6.3)

Records shall include decisions and actions related to:

- Improvement needed to maintain suitability/adequacy/effectiveness of the QMS
- Improvement of product related to customer requirements
- Changes needed to respond to applicable new or revised regulatory requirements
- Resource needs

Each action has an owner and due date, tracked to closure.

### 6.4 Minutes

Management Review minutes are recorded on **GL-FORM-QM-002 — Management Review Minutes**, approved by the Management Representative, and retained as quality records per GL-SOP-QM-001.
