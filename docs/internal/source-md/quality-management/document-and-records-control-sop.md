# GL-SOP-QM-001 — Document and Records Control

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-QM-001
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech
**Supersedes:** None

---

## 1. Purpose

Establish how GlobalLogic controls QMS documents and quality records throughout their lifecycle — creation, review, approval, distribution, change, retention, and retirement — to meet ISO 13485:2016 §4.2.4 and §4.2.5, 21 CFR 820.40, and 21 CFR Part 11 (where applicable).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all controlled documents in the GlobalLogic QMS (Levels 1–5 per **GL-MAN-QM-001 §5.2**) and to all records generated by QMS activities. Includes paper and electronic originals. Supplier-authored documents incorporated into the QMS are governed when referenced by an internal controlled document.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Document Owner | Author, revise, respond to review comments, assign doc_id |
| Quality (QMS Administrator) | Registers doc_id, controls distribution, maintains Master Index |
| Approver | Technical, quality, and (where applicable) regulatory approval |
| Users | Use only the current controlled revision; report errors |

## 4. Definitions

- **Controlled document** — A document listed in the QMS Master Index and managed under this SOP.
- **Effective date** — The date from which users shall execute work per the new revision.
- **Obsolete document** — A superseded or withdrawn document retained for reference under access control.
- **Quality record** — Evidence of conformity to requirements and effective operation of the QMS (ISO 13485 §4.2.5).

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Document Identification

Every controlled document is assigned a Document ID by the QMS Administrator when drafting begins. Format:

```
GL-<TYPE>-<AREA>-<NNN>
```

- `TYPE`: MAN, POL, SOP, WI, FORM, TMP, REC
- `AREA`: QM (quality mgmt), DC (design controls), RM (risk mgmt), SW (software/cyber), UC (usability/clinical), SP (supplier/production), PM (post-market)
- `NNN`: zero-padded sequence within area+type

### 6.2 Document Lifecycle

```
Draft → Technical Review → Quality Review → Approval → Released → (Revision or Obsolete)
```

- **Draft** — author iterating; marked `Revision X.X DRAFT` in header
- **Review** — reviewers comment via tracked changes or review form (GL-FORM-QM-005)
- **Approval** — signed by listed approvers (electronic signature per 21 CFR Part 11 or wet-ink)
- **Released** — entered in Master Index with Effective Date; distributed
- **Revised** — new revision supersedes previous; previous marked Obsolete
- **Obsolete** — removed from active use; retained in archival store with "OBSOLETE" watermark or metadata flag

### 6.3 Change Control

Any change to a released document requires a **Document Change Request (GL-FORM-QM-005)** describing:

- What changes and why
- Risk assessment (impact on product, process, users, regulatory filings)
- Training impact (who must be re-trained)
- Implementation date

Minor editorial changes (typos, formatting) may be processed as administrative changes at the QMS Administrator's discretion; all other changes require full review and approval per §6.2.

### 6.4 Distribution and Access

- **Electronic master** — authoritative; stored in the controlled document repository with access controls, audit trail, and backup
- **Working copies** — downloaded or printed with a "UNCONTROLLED COPY" marking unless the electronic master itself is the copy in use
- **Obsolete copies** — prevented from inadvertent use by access control, watermarking, or physical segregation

### 6.5 Records Control (§4.2.5)

- Records are legible, identifiable, and retrievable
- Record retention period: **minimum 10 years from the date of release of the last device placed on the market** (ISO 13485 §4.2.5; aligned with EU MDR Art. 10(8) for implantable devices — check product-specific retention per Regulatory)
- Records stored electronically shall meet 21 CFR Part 11 for US-regulated records: validated systems, audit trails, access controls, electronic signatures where applicable

### 6.6 Master Index

The QMS Master Index (`qms-index.md` at the root of `docs/internal/source-md/` — see Phase 7 of task ben/022) lists:

| Doc ID | Title | Type | Current Rev | Effective Date | Owner | Supersedes |

Updated whenever a document is released, revised, or retired.
