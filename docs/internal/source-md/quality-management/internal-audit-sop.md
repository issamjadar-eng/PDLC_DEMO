# GL-SOP-QM-004 — Internal Audit

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-QM-004
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Plan, conduct, and report internal audits of the QMS to verify that it conforms to planned arrangements, ISO 13485:2016, 21 CFR Part 820, applicable regulations, and is effectively implemented and maintained — per ISO 13485:2016 §8.2.4 and 21 CFR 820.22.

## 2. Scope

All QMS processes and applicable regulatory requirements. Includes SOP conformance, record completeness, process effectiveness, and follow-up of previous findings.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management Representative (VP Quality) | Approve annual audit program; approve audit reports |
| Lead Auditor | Plan and conduct audits; report findings |
| Auditors | Participate in audits under Lead Auditor direction |
| Auditee (Process Owner) | Provide evidence; respond to findings; close actions |

Auditors shall be **independent of the area being audited**. Auditor qualification: completion of ISO 19011 awareness training plus an observed audit.

## 4. Definitions

- **Audit** — A systematic, independent, documented process for obtaining audit evidence and evaluating it objectively.
- **Finding** — Nonconformity, observation, or opportunity for improvement identified during audit.
- **Nonconformity** — Failure to meet a requirement.

## 5. References

- ISO 13485:2016 §8.2.4
- 21 CFR 820.22
- ISO 19011:2018 Guidelines for auditing management systems

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Audit Program

Annually, the VP Quality issues an Audit Program covering every QMS process at least once per year and every product line at least once per year. Frequency can increase for areas with elevated risk (e.g., post-CAPA areas, supplier quality after NCRs).

### 6.2 Audit Plan (per audit)

The Lead Auditor issues an Audit Plan (GL-FORM-QM-004) at least **10 business days** before the audit. The plan specifies:

- Scope, objectives, criteria
- Auditee(s), date, duration
- Audit team
- Documents to be reviewed in advance

### 6.3 Conduct

- Opening meeting
- Evidence collection (document review, interviews, record sampling, observation)
- Finding discussion with auditee to confirm factual accuracy
- Closing meeting — summary of findings

### 6.4 Findings Classification

| Class | Description |
|---|---|
| **Major NC** | Absence or breakdown of a required QMS process, or a finding that creates a credible risk to safety/efficacy |
| **Minor NC** | A single isolated lapse against a requirement |
| **Observation** | Not a nonconformity; improvement opportunity |

### 6.5 Audit Report

Issued within **10 business days** of closing. Contains scope, audit team, process areas audited, findings with evidence, and required actions. Approved by the Management Representative.

### 6.6 Corrective Action

Nonconformities are processed through CAPA (**GL-SOP-QM-005**). Auditee owns the action; Lead Auditor verifies effectiveness before closure.

### 6.7 Inputs to Management Review

Audit results feed into Management Review (**GL-SOP-QM-002**) as a required input.
