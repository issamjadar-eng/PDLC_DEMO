# GL-SOP-UC-002 — Clinical Evaluation

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-UC-002
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** Clinical Affairs Director, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Establish the process for planning and conducting Clinical Evaluation of GlobalLogic medical devices to demonstrate safety, clinical performance, and clinical benefit — per **EU MDR 2017/745 Art. 61 and Annex XIV**, informed by **MDCG 2020-6** and **MDCG 2020-13**. Where clinical investigation is conducted, align with **ISO 14155:2020** and (for US) **21 CFR Part 812**.

## 2. Scope

All medical devices under the GlobalLogic QMS for which clinical evaluation is required.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Clinical Affairs Director | Own Clinical Evaluation Plan (CEP) and Report (CER) |
| Regulatory Affairs | Determine scope of evidence required per pathway |
| Medical Writer / Clinical Evaluator | Execute literature search and appraisal; draft CER |
| Risk Manager | Integrate clinical data into RMF (GL-SOP-RM-001) |
| PMS Lead | Provide post-market clinical data to lifecycle updates (GL-SOP-PM-001) |

## 4. Definitions

- **Clinical evaluation** — A systematic and planned process to continuously generate, collect, analyze, and assess clinical data pertaining to a device.
- **Clinical data** — Information concerning safety or performance generated from the use of a device; sources include clinical investigations, scientific literature, clinical experience, and PMS.
- **Clinical investigation** — A systematic investigation in one or more human subjects (ISO 14155).
- **State of the art** — The current knowledge or practice regarding products, processes, and services.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Clinical Evaluation Plan (CEP) — GL-TMP-UC-004

Drafted at project initiation (and updated as needed). Contents:

- Device identification and intended use / indications / contraindications / target population
- Equivalence strategy (if claimed) with full substantiation per MDR Annex XIV(3)
- State-of-the-art analysis plan
- Literature search methodology (databases, strategy, inclusion/exclusion)
- Clinical investigation strategy (if needed)
- Clinical data appraisal methodology
- PMS/PMCF inputs plan (link to GL-SOP-PM-001)
- Acceptance criteria for clinical performance / benefit
- Team qualifications

### 6.2 Literature Search and Appraisal

Follow the CEP methodology. All searches are documented for reproducibility: databases, search strings, date, results, inclusions, exclusions with reason. Appraisal uses a documented method (e.g., Oxford CEBM, OCEBM 2011, PRISMA adherence for reviews). A Literature Search Report accompanies the CER.

### 6.3 Clinical Investigations (where applicable)

Design per ISO 14155:2020. Jurisdictional requirements followed (IDE for US, Competent-Authority notifications for EU, ethics committee approvals). Investigator brochure and protocol controlled under this SOP and the QMS document control (GL-SOP-QM-001).

### 6.4 Clinical Evaluation Report (CER) — GL-TMP-UC-005

The CER (template GL-TMP-UC-005) follows MDCG 2020-13 structure and documents:

- Scope and intended purpose
- State of the art and alternatives
- Equivalence substantiation (if any)
- Clinical data (investigations, literature, clinical experience, PMS)
- Appraisal and benefit-risk analysis
- Conclusions on safety, performance, clinical benefit
- Residual risks, uncertainties, limitations
- PMCF plan reference

### 6.5 Approvals

CER is approved by Clinical Affairs Director, VP Regulatory, VP Quality, and the Medical Device Safety Officer / Person Responsible for Regulatory Compliance (Art. 15) before submission or release.

### 6.6 Lifecycle Updates

CER is updated:
- Annually for Class III and implantables
- Every 2–5 years for other devices per risk
- On significant change (GL-SOP-DC-008), substantive post-market signal (GL-SOP-PM-001), or new state-of-the-art publication
