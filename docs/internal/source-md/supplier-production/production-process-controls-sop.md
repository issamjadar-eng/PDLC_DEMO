# GL-SOP-SP-003 — Production and Process Controls

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-SP-003
**Revision:** 1.0
**Owner:** VP Operations, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Control production processes and environments — per ISO 13485:2016 §7.5 and 21 CFR 820.70. Define process validation per 21 CFR 820.75 and GHTF/SG3/N99-10:2004.

## 2. Scope

All production and service-provision activities for GlobalLogic medical devices.

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Controlled Conditions (§7.5.1)

- Documented procedures / Work Instructions (GL-WI-*)
- Equipment qualified (IQ/OQ) and calibrated
- Monitoring and measuring at defined points
- Approved materials and components (ASL — GL-SOP-SP-001)
- Device identification and traceability (§7.5.8, §7.5.9)
- Labeling control (21 CFR 820.120)
- DMR drives the shop floor (21 CFR 820.181)
- DHR per lot / unit (21 CFR 820.184)

### 3.2 Cleanliness and Contamination Control (§7.5.2)

Per product risk — e.g., sterile devices follow ISO 13408/11135 validation requirements and cleanroom controls per ISO 14644.

### 3.3 Installation Activities (§7.5.3) and Servicing (§7.5.4)

Documented per product when applicable.

### 3.4 Process Validation (§7.5.6 / 21 CFR 820.75)

Processes whose output cannot be fully verified by subsequent inspection and testing shall be validated. Validation approach per GHTF/SG3/N99-10:

- **IQ (Installation Qualification)** — equipment installed correctly
- **OQ (Operational Qualification)** — process performs within defined limits
- **PQ (Performance Qualification)** — repeatable output meets spec

Revalidation triggers: significant process change, equipment change, facility move, unresolved trend.

### 3.5 Monitoring and Measurement

SPC where applicable; control limits defined per process; reaction plans on out-of-control signals. Data feed Management Review (GL-SOP-QM-002).

### 3.6 Monitoring and Measuring Equipment (§7.6)

Calibrated against traceable standards; records retained; out-of-tolerance analysis assesses impact on product.

## 4. Records Generated

- Process validation (IQ/OQ/PQ) records — per process
- DMR (per product)
- DHR (per unit / lot)
- Calibration records
