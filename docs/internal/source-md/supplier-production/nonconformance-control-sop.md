# GL-SOP-SP-004 — Control of Nonconforming Product

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-SP-004
**Revision:** 1.0
**Owner:** VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Identify and control product that does not conform to requirements — per ISO 13485:2016 §8.3 and 21 CFR 820.90.

## 2. Procedure

### 2.1 Identification

Any employee identifying a nonconformance shall:
- Stop work on the affected unit/lot
- Segregate or tag as "HOLD — NCR pending"
- Raise a Nonconformance Report (GL-FORM-SP-002)

### 2.2 Evaluation

Quality Engineering + Design (as needed) investigates root cause and impact on:
- Safety / efficacy
- Other units / lots / fields
- Risk Management File (GL-SOP-RM-001)
- Regulatory reportability (GL-SOP-PM-003)

### 2.3 Disposition (§8.3.2)

Options:
- **Accept-as-is with justification** — requires documented rationale and, where applicable, concession approval
- **Rework** — per approved rework instruction; re-verification required
- **Repair** — same as rework but reducing the product to a lower spec; usage impact documented
- **Scrap** — product destroyed; records retained

### 2.4 Released Product (§8.3.3)

For nonconforming product that has already been released / shipped, the disposition shall include an assessment of the need for:
- Customer notification
- Field correction (recall, advisory)
- Regulatory reporting (21 CFR 806, EU MDR Art. 87)
- CAPA (GL-SOP-QM-005)

### 2.5 Rework (§8.3.4)

Rework shall not adversely affect product. Rework procedure reviewed and approved; re-verification performed.

## 3. Records Generated

- NCR form (GL-FORM-SP-002)
- Disposition records
- Rework procedures and records
