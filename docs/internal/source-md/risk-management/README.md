## Conventions

- Doc IDs follow `GL-<TYPE>-RM-<NNN>`.
- Risk acceptability defined per product in the RM Plan (§3 of the template). Do not reuse scales across products without review.
- The RMF cross-references Usability (`../usability-clinical/`), Software (`../software-cybersecurity/`), Clinical (`../usability-clinical/`), and Design Controls (`../design-controls/`).
