# GL-SOP-PM-001 — Post-Market Surveillance (PMS)

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Document ID:** GL-SOP-PM-001
**Revision:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-21
**Owner:** PMS Lead / VP Quality, GlobalLogic MedTech

---

## 1. Purpose

Plan and conduct Post-Market Surveillance (PMS) for GlobalLogic medical devices — systematically and proactively collecting, recording, and analyzing data about the quality, performance, and safety of devices in use — per **EU MDR Art. 83–86 + Annex III**, informed by **MDCG 2022-21** (PSUR), and aligned with **ISO 13485:2016 §8.2.1**.

## 2. Scope

All devices placed on the market by GlobalLogic MedTech. Includes devices under FDA-mandated postmarket surveillance under 21 CFR Part 822 where applicable.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **PMS Lead** — own PMS Plan and Reports; chair PMS review board
- **Complaint Handling Lead** — feed data (GL-SOP-PM-002)
- **Vigilance / Regulatory** — adverse-event reporting (GL-SOP-PM-003)
- **Clinical Affairs** — PMCF execution
- **Risk Manager** — integrate into RMF (GL-SOP-RM-001)

## 4. Definitions

- **PMS System** — Systematic procedures to pro-actively collect and review experience gained from devices placed on the market (MDR Art. 83).
- **PMCF** — Post-Market Clinical Follow-up.
- **PMS Report** — Report summarizing PMS outputs; for Class I devices per MDR.
- **PSUR** — Periodic Safety Update Report; for Class IIa/IIb/III devices per MDR Art. 86.

## 5. References

- EU MDR 2017/745 Art. 83–86; Annex III (PMS Plan contents), Annex XIV Part B (PMCF)
- MDCG 2022-21 PSUR guidance
- MDCG 2020-7 PMCF guidance
- ISO 13485:2016 §8.2.1 Feedback
- ISO/TR 20416:2020 — Post-market surveillance for manufacturers
- 21 CFR Part 822 (US postmarket surveillance, as applicable)

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 PMS Plan (Annex III §1.1)

Drafted at project initiation; part of the DHF. Contents:

- Processes for collecting and using experience in the PMS database
- PMCF plan (Annex XIV Part B) — or justification for non-applicability
- Complaint and trend monitoring methods (GL-SOP-PM-002)
- Process for communicating with competent authorities, notified bodies, users (GL-SOP-PM-003)
- Procedures for the Field Safety Corrective Action (FSCA) process
- Process for managing vigilance trends and reportable events (serious incidents)
- Methods to measure and trend benefit-risk in the post-market phase

### 6.2 Data Sources

- Complaints and feedback (GL-SOP-PM-002)
- Warranty / return data
- Service records
- Competitor/similar-device literature and field actions
- Regulator databases (FDA MAUDE, Eudamed, Health Canada, etc.)
- Scientific literature
- Vigilance reports (GL-SOP-PM-003)
- User surveys / clinical feedback
- PMCF studies (Annex XIV Part B)

### 6.3 Analysis and Trending

PMS Lead trends at defined cadence (typically quarterly). Investigates signals for:
- Emerging hazards not in the Risk Management File
- Changes in frequency or severity of known risks
- User errors and usability issues
- Performance degradation
- Unanticipated use / off-label use

### 6.4 Outputs

- **PMS Report** (Class I) per Annex III — available on request to competent authorities
- **PSUR** (Class IIa / IIb / III) per Art. 86 — updated at defined intervals (Class IIa ≥ every 2 years, Class IIb / III at least annually)
- **Updates to Risk Management File** (GL-SOP-RM-001) and **CER** (GL-SOP-UC-002)
- **CAPA inputs** (GL-SOP-QM-005)
- **Design change** proposals (GL-SOP-DC-008)
- **Field Safety Corrective Action (FSCA)** where warranted, with Field Safety Notice (FSN) to users/customers

### 6.5 Management Review Input

PMS outputs are a required Management Review input (GL-SOP-QM-002).
