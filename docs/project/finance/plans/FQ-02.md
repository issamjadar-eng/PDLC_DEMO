## Question

Price / volume / mix bridge — how much of the revenue and margin change vs prior year is price, how much is volume, how much is mix?

**Asked by**: cfo · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ The pricing-vs-volume decision behind FQ-01's erosion
findings: a line losing margin on price needs a pricing action; one losing on volume
needs a demand or run-down decision; one losing on mix needs a portfolio decision.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- Bridge each line's revenue change vs the prior-year window into **volume** (Δ units ×
  prior-year ASP), **price** (Δ ASP × current units) and **mix** (shift of revenue-type
  and line weights at constant ASPs), summing exactly to the revenue change.
- Margin bridge adds a **cost** term from the standard-cost variance (FQ-01).
- ASP = revenue ÷ units per (quarter, line, revenue type) from the financials — the
  only unit basis available; consumables ASP is per consumable unit, service per
  contract-quarter.
