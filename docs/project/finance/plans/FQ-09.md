## Question

Subscription economics — ARR, net revenue retention, and churn for the cloud suite; is recurring revenue compounding or leaking?

**Asked by**: cfo, board · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ Whether the cloud-suite subscription base is a
compounding asset (retention above 100%, low churn) or a leaking one — the board's
recurring-revenue quality question, complementing BQ-28's recurring-revenue trajectory.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- **ARR** = annualized subscription revenue at the latest quarter (from the financials'
  subscription rows or the subscriptions register, reconciled).
- **Net revenue retention** = revenue at the latest quarter from customers active one
  year earlier ÷ their revenue one year earlier; **churn** = customers (or ARR) lost
  over the year ÷ the starting base.
- Cohort anchor = the subscriptions register's start / end dates.
