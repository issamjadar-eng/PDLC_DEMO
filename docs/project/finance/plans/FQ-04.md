## Question

Warranty and service reserve adequacy vs the observed field failure rate — is the reserve keeping up with what the field is costing?

**Asked by**: controller, vp-quality · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ The quarterly reserve true-up: whether the warranty
reserve on the balance sheet covers what the observed claim rate and cost per claim
imply for devices still under warranty, per line.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- **Observed claim rate** = claims per installed unit per month from the warranty log
  over a trailing window, per line / revision (denominator: fleet registry).
- **Expected remaining cost** = rate × cost per claim × units × remaining warranty
  months; compared with the reserve balance per line.
- Adequacy band and the trailing-window length are plan constants.
