# Analysis plan — FQ-03

_The analysis contract for this question: what the answer is FOR, how it is computed,

## Question

Cost of poor quality — warranty spend, warranty cost per installed unit by line and hardware revision, share linked to complaints; trend?

**Asked by**: vp-quality, cfo · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly quality-cost review: whether a hardware revision needs a design-signal
review or field action, whether a legacy line's per-unit warranty cost warrants a
reserve re-test (FQ-04) and end-of-life timing, and whether warranty activity is
reaching the complaint-handling system. The failure mode this question catches is a
flat warranty total hiding a revision cluster, or fleet growth hiding a worsening
per-unit cost.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **YTD window** = the months of the configured `fy` present in the pinned
  `finance/internal-warranty-claims` snapshot (claim_date month); **prior** = the same
  month labels one year earlier. The as-of month is the latest claim month in the pin —
  never the machine date.
- **Warranty spend** = Σ `cost_usd` over claims in the window (all dispositions;
  denied claims carry zero cost by construction).
- **Per installed unit** = window spend ÷ device count in the pinned
  `commercial/internal-fleet` registry, per model (line level) and per (model, hw_rev)
  **only where the registry carries that revision**. The registry is point-in-time and
  is applied to both windows (a stated limitation carried as a risk item). Lines the
  registry does not carry, and revisions it records as unknown (`-`), are published as
  an unavailable series — never approximated from claim counts.
- **Revision concentration** = for each line with more than one revision among its
  window claims, each revision's share of the line's claims **against its share of the
  installed fleet** (over-index ratio = claim share ÷ installed share). A dominant
  revision naturally carries most claims, so the raw share is never the finding.
  Over-index beyond `rev_overindex_ratio` (1.25×) is the E-03.2 finding — computable
  only for lines whose revision split the registry carries. Where the registry records
  no revision for a line, a revision carrying more than `rev_concentration_pct` (50%)
  of claims is reported as a **not-normalizable concentration** (an issue naming the
  registry gap, not an expectation verdict). Failure categories are listed for every
  flagged revision and charted for the most over-indexed one.
- **Complaint linkage** = share of window claims with `linked_complaint = yes`, overall
  and per line; context = complaints opened in the same months in
  `commercial/internal-complaints` for the models the fleet carries.
- **Trend** = monthly warranty cost per line over the full claims history (the three
  largest lines by total cost individually, the rest combined); disposition mix in
  dollars for the window.
- **PP3500 per-unit YoY** = window per-unit cost vs prior-year per-unit cost, flagged
  beyond `per_unit_yoy_tolerance_pct` (E-03.1).
- Compare on unrounded values; round only at render.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `finance/internal-warranty-claims` (per-claim log: line, region, hw_rev, date, category, cost, disposition, linkage)
- `commercial/internal-fleet` (installed-base registry: model, hw_rev per device — the ONLY per-unit denominator)
- `commercial/internal-complaints` (complaint log — context for linkage)

Data we still need (stated gaps): fleet registry rows for IP5000 / SP6000 / SP6500 and
a recorded PP3000 hardware revision (both block per-unit figures); per-window fleet
snapshots (to replace the point-in-time denominator); the warranty reserve balance
(FQ-04).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-03.1** — Warranty cost per installed PP3500 unit does not rise year over year
  beyond tolerance _(basis: quality-plan reliability target carried forward; no approved
  KPI threshold on file [VERIFY])_. Not-evaluable if PP3500 has no denominator or no
  prior-year claims.
- **E-03.2** — No hardware revision concentrates a line's warranty claims _(basis:
  design-signal concentration guardrail; not a risk-file threshold [VERIFY])_.
- **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline carries spend vs prior year, per-unit
  cost for every line with a denominator, the top concentration finding, and the
  linkage share — a verdict-only rollup cannot drop the revision cluster.
- The claims generator is seeded to cluster PP3000 on rev B and to raise legacy cost;
  its corpus assert proves those knobs — the answer must still compute them.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: warranty spend in the window vs prior year, per-unit cost where a
fleet denominator exists, revision concentration per line, complaint-linkage share, and
the monthly cost trend. It does **not** assert: a field failure RATE (needs exposure
time, not a point-in-time fleet), reserve adequacy (FQ-04), root cause of the
concentrated failure categories, or whether a field action is required — it triggers
the design-signal review, it does not conclude it.
