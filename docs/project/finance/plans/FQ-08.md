## Question

Budget vs actual year to date by function with headcount variance — which functions breach the tolerance and why?

**Asked by**: controller, cfo · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly close: which functions need a re-plan or re-phase, whether an over-spend is
buying a milestone, and whether an under-spend is savings or a hiring lag deferring
work the revenue plan depends on. The failure mode this question catches is a
within-tolerance company total netting an R&D overrun against a sales hiring lag.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **YTD window** = every `period` in the pinned `finance/internal-gl-budget` snapshot;
  the computation asserts that all periods belong to the configured `fy` and fails loud
  otherwise. Headcount is read at the latest period.
- **Variance** = (Σ actual − Σ budget) ÷ Σ budget per function and in total, over the
  window. **Breach** = |variance| > `tolerance_pct` (5%) per function; the total is read
  against `total_tolerance_pct` (3%). Both are stand-ins (E-08.1, E-08.2).
- **Why (computed, never narrated)**: for each breaching function, the cost center with
  the largest absolute dollar variance is named as the driver, with all its cost
  centers tabled; for an under-spend where latest-period headcount actual < budget, the
  headcount shortfall is named as a hiring lag; for an over-spend with headcount actual
  > budget, the head excess is named.
- **Trend** = monthly variance % for the total and the three functions with the largest
  |YTD variance|.
- **Spend by project: NOT IN THE LEDGER.** Published as an unavailable series; program
  attribution and capitalization are FQ-10.
- Compare on unrounded values; round only at render.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-08.1** — Every function's year-to-date spend is within tolerance of budget
  _(basis: standard variance-review trigger; the budget book names no tolerance
  [VERIFY])_.
- **E-08.2** — Total operating spend year to date is within the company tolerance
  _(basis: board reporting convention carried forward; not in the budget book
  [VERIFY])_.
- **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline carries the total vs budget and every
  breaching function with its variance and computed driver, so a verdict-only rollup
  cannot drop the netting inside the total.
- The GL generator is seeded with R&D and Regulatory/Quality over and Sales &
  Marketing under; its corpus assert proves those knobs — the answer must still compute
  the breaches and drivers from the data.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: YTD variance per function and in total, which functions breach the
band, the cost center driving each breach, and the headcount position at the latest
period. It does **not** assert: whether an overrun is buying a milestone (FQ-10), a
full-year landing, savings (an under-spend with missing heads is deferred work), or a
reforecast — it triggers the re-plan conversation, it does not conclude it.
