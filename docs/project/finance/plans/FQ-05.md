## Question

Cost of regulatory delay — per month, per product line, what does a slipped clearance cost in deferred revenue and carried spend?

**Asked by**: cfo, board · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ Weighing the cost of a submission slip against the cost
of accelerating it: a per-month dollar figure per line the board can act on.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- **Deferred revenue per month** = the plan-of-record revenue phased to the months after
  the planned clearance, per line (from the revenue plan), for each month of slip.
- **Carried spend per month** = the program's run-rate spend (R&D + Regulatory/Quality
  cost centers attributable to the line) that continues during the slip.
- The slip scenario length is a plan constant; no probability weighting.
