## Question

Gross margin by product line and region vs prior year, with standard-vs-actual cost variance by line — which lines are eroding?

**Asked by**: cfo, board · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly margin review: which product lines get a cost review (standard reset,
supplier action, run-down) and which get a pricing conversation, and whether the
company margin story the board hears is line health or mix. The failure mode this
question exists to catch is an aggregate margin that holds or rises while legacy lines
erode underneath it — and margin reporting on stale standards that flatters those lines.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Window** = every closed quarter of the configured `fy` present in the pinned
  `commercial/internal-financials` snapshot (currently H1: Q1 + Q2). **Prior year** = the
  same quarter labels one year earlier. No partial-quarter extrapolation.
- **Gross margin** = (revenue − COGS) ÷ revenue, computed per product line (summed over
  regions and revenue types), per region (summed over lines), and in total. Region and
  line cuts use the same window.
- **Eroding** = a line whose gross margin moved by `erosion_threshold_pts` or worse year
  over year (−1.0 pt). The company floor is `gm_floor_pct` (55%) — a stand-in (E-01.1).
- **Actual unit cost** = COGS ÷ units per (quarter, line, revenue type), summed over
  regions from the financials. **Standard** = `std_unit_cost_usd` for the same
  (quarter, line, revenue type) in the pinned `finance/internal-standard-costs`
  snapshot. **Variance** = (actual − standard) × units summed over the window per line,
  expressed as % of standard cost; **positive = unfavorable**. A cell with no standard is
  excluded and counted in a watch item — never zero-filled.
- **Breach** = |variance %| > `cost_variance_tolerance_pct` (5%) in either direction
  (a favorable breach is also a stale standard), tested on the line total (all revenue
  types) for E-01.2. The **hardware-only variance** per line is reported alongside and
  flagged against the same tolerance as the leading indicator — the line total dilutes
  it with consumables and service, which carry small stable gaps.
- **Trend** = quarterly gross margin over the full history in the pin (company + the
  three largest lines by window revenue) and quarterly hardware unit-cost variance per
  line.
- **Variance by region: NOT COMPUTABLE.** Standards carry no region dimension; published
  as an unavailable series, never approximated from regional COGS alone.
- Compare on unrounded values; round only at render.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-financials` (quarterly revenue, COGS, units by line × region × revenue type)
- `finance/internal-standard-costs` (standard unit cost by quarter × line × revenue type, with M/L/O split)

Data we have: everything above. Data we still need (stated gaps): regional standards or
a regional actual-cost feed (blocks variance by region); a price/volume/mix bridge is
FQ-02's job, not this answer's.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-01.1** — Company gross margin holds at or above the floor over the fiscal-year
  window _(basis: board-deck target carried forward; no plan of record names a floor
  [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule: met if company GM ≥ floor. **Rollup-survival rule**: the
  verdict headline and the E-01.1 actual both carry the eroding-line count
  ("N of M lines eroding") so a verdict-only rollup cannot drop line erosion.
- **E-01.2** — No product line's standard-cost variance exceeds the review tolerance
  _(basis: cost-accounting review trigger; no controller policy names a tolerance
  [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule: met if no line breaches ±tolerance.
- Both datasets are demo-fabricated; the standard-costs generator is seeded to run
  legacy hardware unfavorable and PP3500 favorable, and its corpus assert proves that
  against the financials snapshot — the answer must still compute the variance, not
  assume it.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: gross margin per line / region / company for the window vs prior
year, which lines erode past the threshold, and the standard-vs-actual cost variance per
line with its breach status. It does **not** assert: a regional cost variance, a
price/volume/mix explanation (FQ-02), root cause of any erosion (needs supplier and
pricing data), or a margin forecast — it names the lines for review, it does not
conclude the review.
