## Question

Consumable pull-through vs installed base — are consumable shipments per installed device holding, by line and region?

**Asked by**: coo, cfo · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ Spotting idle devices and consumable leakage (customers
sourcing elsewhere) early enough for the sales and service teams to act, by line and
region.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- **Pull-through** = consumable units shipped per month ÷ installed devices, per line ×
  region; trend over the full cube history.
- **Denominator** = the fleet registry per model × region (point-in-time; per-window
  snapshots when available). Lines the registry does not carry are stated gaps.
- A pull-through floor per line is a plan constant (stand-in until the commercial plan
  names one).
