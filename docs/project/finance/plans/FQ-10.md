## Question

R&D spend vs milestone — what has each program consumed against its plan, and what is eligible for capitalization?

**Asked by**: cfo, coo · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

_Roadmap — not yet implemented._ Whether an R&D overrun (FQ-08) is buying a milestone or
not, and the capitalization number the auditors will ask for at year end.

## Approach

Planned definitions (to be committed when implemented):

- **Program spend** = actuals per program per month from a project-dimensioned ledger
  feed, cumulative to each milestone date.
- **Milestone plan** = the spend phased to each milestone in the program plan; variance
  at the milestone, not just at the period.
- **Capitalization-eligible** = spend after the technical-feasibility milestone per the
  accounting policy on file — the policy citation is required before any figure is
  published.
