## Question

Top three complaint categories this quarter, rate-normalized with a stated denominator —
any trending above the risk-file commitment?

**Asked by**: vp-quality, ceo · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The management-review CAPA trigger: whether any complaint category's rate obligates a
CAPA review this cycle, with a denominator the reviewer can defend. A breach obligates
a REVIEW, not automatically a CAPA.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Window** = trailing 90 days ending at the latest complaint date in the pinned
  snapshot; the preceding 90 days is the comparison window.
- **Rate** = complaints per 100 fleet devices; the denominator is the installed-base
  registry — the ONLY sanctioned internal denominator (per corpus conventions), and its
  size is stated in the report.
- **Thresholds** come from commercial.yml and are demo stand-ins — they MUST be
  re-derived from the risk file's acceptability criteria before real use; every verdict
  against them is flagged unvalidated.
- **Trend rule** = current window > 130% of prior window raises a rising-volume risk.
- **Historical view** = monthly counts for the top-3 categories.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-complaints`
- `commercial/internal-fleet`

Data we have: demo-fabricated complaint log + fleet registry (stand-ins for the
complaint-handling system and installed-base exports). Data we still need (stated gaps,
never faked): risk-file-derived thresholds; severity weighting and per-site/firmware
stratification are computed inputs for a CAPA review, not part of this answer.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-18.1 / E-18.2** as declared in commercial.yml — both unvalidated stand-ins.
- The fleet registry is assumed current enough (7-day cadence) to serve as denominator.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: category rates against stated thresholds with a stated denominator,
and the window-over-window volume trend. It does **not** assert: root cause, severity
mix, site attribution, or that a breach IS a CAPA — it obligates the review. Demo data
throughout; not for clinical use.
