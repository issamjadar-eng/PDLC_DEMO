## Question

How far behind current is the fleet — % of pumps more than one firmware version behind,
at which accounts, and connected vs not?

**Asked by**: customer-success · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly currency posture: which accounts get proactive campaign outreach vs
waiting for the next PM cycle — with the safety framing that an outdated
drug-error-reduction library is patient-safety exposure, not just an ops metric.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Behind-by** mapping comes from commercial.yml (current / one behind / two behind);
  scope is the PP3500 fleet, with legacy PP3000 counted separately, never mixed in.
- **Connectivity comparison** (current-version % for connected vs unconnected) is
  DESCRIPTIVE only — no causal claim that connectivity drives currency (adversarial
  verification noted the direction can even run the other way).
- **Historical view**: one fleet snapshot is NOT a trend — the currency-over-time
  series stays a stated gap until the dataset's 7-day refresh cadence accumulates
  snapshots. No history is faked from a single point.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-fleet`

Data we have: per-device model/firmware/connectivity registry (demo-fabricated
stand-in for the fleet-management export). Data we still need (stated gaps, never
faked): multiple snapshots over time (accumulating); drug-library revision status per
pump for the DERS-staleness cut; per-account outreach history.

## Assumptions & expectations

- The registry is assumed complete for the installed base; unconnected devices' state
  is as of their last service contact and may lag.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the fleet's current version posture by region and connectivity,
against the declared behind-by mapping. It does **not** assert: why an account is
behind, a currency trend (yet), or a causal connectivity effect. Demo data; not for
clinical use.
