## Question

What are our update failure and retry rates — is any hardware rev, firmware baseline, or
site profile failing at a rate that says pause and escalate?

**Asked by**: vp-field-service · **cadence**: weekly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The pause/continue trigger for an active campaign: stop the wave for a failing cohort
and open an engineering investigation before more devices are affected. The trigger
must be honest about its denominator — diluting failures with never-attempted devices
understates severity.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Attempt failure** = status `completed-after-retry`, `failed-pending-retry`, or
  `rolled-back`.
- **Primary rate** = failures per ATTEMPTED device (attempted excludes
  still-`scheduled`); the whole-cohort rate is shown for context only. This basis was
  adopted after adversarial verification showed the whole-cohort basis understates
  severity.
- **Cohort** = hardware rev × from-version; **pause rule** = per-attempt rate above the
  commercial.yml threshold with attempted n ≥ the minimum cohort size. The threshold is
  a demo stand-in (E-24.1, unvalidated) — it must be re-derived from the risk file.
- **Historical view** = weekly completions vs completions-that-needed-retry, dated
  events only — still-pending failures have no date and are excluded, stated not hidden.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-upgrade-campaign`

Data we have: per-device attempt outcomes by cohort. Data we still need (stated gaps,
never faked): a risk-file-derived pause threshold; failure root-cause data (engineering
investigation output, not campaign telemetry).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-24.1** as declared — unvalidated stand-in threshold.
- Cohort membership (hw_rev × from_version) is assumed the right first-cut failure
  stratification; site profile is a secondary cut not asserted here.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: per-attempt failure rates by cohort, whether any cohort trips the
stated pause rule, and the weekly failure trend among dated events. It does **not**
assert: root cause, fix scope, or resume criteria — those belong to the engineering
investigation the trigger opens. Demo data; not for clinical use.
