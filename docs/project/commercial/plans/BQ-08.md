## Question

What are we winning and losing on — and how many recent losses cite predictive monitoring we don't have?

**Asked by**: cco · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The CCO's quarterly competitive-selling read: which loss reasons dominate the recent
book (pricing action vs feature roadmap vs relationship coverage), and specifically how
much lost revenue is attributable to the predictive-monitoring gap — the input that
feeds the Y3 predictive-monitoring investment case (BQ-13/BQ-29 adjacency).

## Approach

Committed definitions:

- **Window**: trailing 365 days anchored at the newest `close_date` in the pinned
  snapshot (deterministic against the pin; no wall clock). `window_days: 365` from
  `commercial.yml`.
- **Decided** = outcome `won` or `lost`. `no-decision` opportunities are excluded from
  the win-rate denominator and reported separately — counting them as losses would
  understate the rate; hiding them would overstate pipeline health.
- **Win rate** = won ÷ (won + lost), decided in-window. Judged against **E-08.1**
  (≥ 50% of decided) — the catalog's stand-in target.
- **Dollar-weighted win rate** = Σ `value_usd` over in-window wins ÷ Σ `value_usd`
  over in-window decided (won + lost) — reported beside the count-based rate so a
  lose-bigger-deals-than-we-win pattern cannot hide behind a healthy count rate.
- **Denominator sensitivity (committed)**: E-08.1 is judged on the count-based decided
  rate, but the report states the no-decisions-as-losses rate next to the verdict —
  the reader must see when "met" hangs on the denominator choice, not just on sample
  size.
- **Loss-reason breakdown** = `primary_reason` counts over in-window losses (one
  primary reason per opportunity — the CRM's attribution, taken as recorded).
- **Predictive-monitoring-gap losses** = in-window losses where `primary_reason ==
  predictive-monitoring-gap` **OR** `cites_predictive_monitoring == yes` (primary or
  cited — the wider, honest definition; the primary-only count is also reported).
- **Revenue value of PM-gap losses** = sum of `value_usd` over that set — opportunity
  value as recorded in CRM, not discounted for win probability.
- **History**: monthly decided counts (won line, lost line, PM-gap-loss line) across
  the full snapshot span, zero-filled so quiet months show as zero, not gaps.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-winloss` — demo-fabricated CRM opportunity log (~120 opps,
  2025-01..2026-07; real competitor names inside fabricated records).

Data we have: per-opportunity outcome, primary reason, PM citation flag, value. Data we
do NOT have: reason attribution quality (a single primary reason per opp is a CRM
simplification), buyer-side verbatims, and any competitor's win/loss book. Loss-reason
findings are our-CRM-as-recorded, not market truth.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-08.1** — Competitive win rate holds at or above half of decided opportunities
  _(basis: sales-plan stand-in; no documented target — unvalidated, challengeable)_.
- The seeded narrative expects ~45% all-time win rate with ~30% of losses touching the
  PM gap; the trailing-365d window may legitimately differ from the all-time figure —
  the window's number is the answer, the seed note is context only.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the in-window win rate vs E-08.1, the loss-reason mix, and the
count/share/value of PM-gap losses as recorded in our CRM. It does NOT assert: why the
market chose a competitor beyond the CRM's single-reason attribution, deal-level
causality (a cited gap is not proof the gap decided the deal), or any forecast of the
win rate. Value-at-loss is CRM opportunity value, not bookable revenue.
