## Question

Which of our five device lines fund the company and which consume it — revenue, margin, trajectory by line?

**Asked by**: board-chair · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The board's quarterly portfolio-allocation read: which lines generate the gross-margin
dollars that fund the program (and how concentrated that funding is), which lines are
margin-thin or margin-negative, and whether any line's trajectory says its role is
changing. It equips resource-allocation and phase-out conversations; it does not make
them.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Product lines** = the six lines in the financial actuals: the five device lines
  (IP5000, PP3000, PP3500, SP6000, SP6500) plus `cloud-suite` (subscription-only).
  The question says "five device lines"; cloud-suite is included because its
  gross-margin dollars are part of who-funds-whom and excluding it would hide the
  recurring engine.
- **Window** = the trailing four quarters (`trailing_quarters` in `commercial.yml`),
  anchored at the **latest period present in the pinned snapshot** — no clocks. With
  the current snapshot that is 2025-Q3..2026-Q2.
- **Gross margin** = revenue − COGS, summed over the window per line, across all
  regions and revenue types. **GM%** = GM$ ÷ revenue. No opex allocation exists in the
  dataset, so "funds vs consumes" is a **gross-margin** statement only.
- **Funds vs consumes**: a line *funds* the company in proportion to its share of
  total trailing-4Q gross-margin dollars; a line *consumes* if its trailing-4Q GM$ is
  negative (the E-01.1 guardrail). Margin-thin-and-shrinking lines are flagged in the
  narrative even when GM-positive.
- **Trajectory** = quarterly revenue per line over the full snapshot history, and
  quarter-over-quarter GM% drift over the window. For the trend chart the ≤4-line
  dataviz cap is respected by charting the top `timeseries_top_lines` (= 3) lines by
  trailing-4Q revenue individually and aggregating the remaining three as one
  "other" line — the aggregation is stated on the chart label.
- Quarter labels map to ISO dates as the first day of the calendar quarter
  (2026-Q2 → 2026-04-01) for timeseries x-axes.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-financials` — quarterly revenue/COGS/units by line, region,
  revenue type (demo-fabricated, seeded generator; stand-in for the ERP export).

Data we have: everything needed for revenue, gross margin, and trajectory by line.
Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked): operating-expense allocation by line —
without it, "consumes the company" can only be asserted at gross-margin level, and a
line that is GM-positive but opex-heavy (R&D, salesforce) is invisible here; and units
economics per line (ASP/unit-cost trends) which this answer does not attempt.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-01.1** — Every product line is gross-margin positive over the trailing four
  quarters _(basis: generic portfolio guardrail; no board-adopted floor — unvalidated,
  challengeable)_.
- No estimation is performed: every figure is measured from the pinned snapshot or
  arithmetically derived from it (shares, GM%, deltas), with derivations declared.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: trailing-4Q revenue, GM$ and GM% per line, each line's share of
total gross margin, the quarterly revenue trajectory, and whether E-01.1 holds. It
does **not** assert: operating profitability or "strategic worth" of a line (no opex
data), a phase-out recommendation, forward projections of any line's revenue, or
consolidated-company profitability. Demo-fabricated data — directional narrative only.
