## Question

What is our actual share of the US PCA/smart-pump segment — taking share from BD/Baxter or growing with the market?

**Asked by**: cco · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The CCO's quarterly market-position read: is the commercial motion taking share or
merely riding market growth, and how should the answer's precision (or lack of it) be
represented to the board? This is deliberately an **honesty-showcase answer**: no
public per-segment unit census exists, so the entire share figure rests on the A-003
market-size assumption — the assumption IS the headline caveat, and the answer must
present share as a range with the denominator's confidence stated, never as a point.

## Approach

Committed definitions:

- **US proxy = NA region.** The fabricated sales dataset carries region (NA/EMEA/APAC),
  not country. NA is used as the US proxy and every share figure is labeled as such.
- **In-segment product lines = PP3500 + PP3000 (PCA) + IP5000 (large-volume smart
  pump)** — matching A-003's scope (US large-volume + PCA smart pumps). Syringe lines
  (SP6000/SP6500) and the cloud-suite subscription line are excluded from the segment
  numerator.
- **Unit share** = NA in-segment `units_installed` per FY ÷ the A-003 installed-base
  range (1.1M–1.8M units) ⇒ a share RANGE per FY. **Revenue share** = NA in-segment
  revenue ÷ the A-003 segment dollar range ($1.5B–$3.0B/yr); FY2026H1 revenue is
  straight-line annualized (×2) for that comparison only, and labeled as annualized.
- **Denominator held constant across FYs.** A-003 is a current-state estimate; applying
  it to FY2024–FY2026 ignores ~7%/yr market growth — stated as a limit, acceptable at
  order-of-magnitude precision.
- **Taking-share vs growing-with-market** is answered via growth-rate comparison: our
  NA in-segment installed-base growth vs the A-003 market growth rate (~7.3% CAGR). At
  our share magnitude (<0.1%), share-shift against BD/Baxter is not observable in any
  data we hold; the honest claim is a growth-rate differential from a tiny base.
  **The two rates are on mismatched bases** — ours is UNIT growth of our own NA
  in-segment installed base; A-003's ~7.3% is the DOLLAR CAGR of the TOTAL US
  infusion-pump market (not unit growth, not segment-scoped). The comparison is
  directional only, and the report labels the mismatch wherever "directionally
  gaining" is claimed.
- **Competitor unit split** (BD vs Baxter vs ICU installed units): no public source —
  published as an `unavailable` series naming what would fill it, never estimated.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-sales-accounts` — our NA units + revenue by product line and FY
  (demo-fabricated; direct book only — hardware + subscription).

Data we have: our own installed units and revenue, FY2024/FY2025/FY2026H1. Data we do
NOT have: any competitor unit census, any segment unit census — both are absorbed into
the A-003 range (assumption record, confidence: low) or marked unavailable.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **A-003** — US smart-pump segment size (installed base 1.1M–1.8M units; segment
  dollar market $1.5B–$3.0B/yr). Confidence LOW; triangulated from public analyst-report
  summaries. Every share figure carries `[assume: A-003]` and evidence class
  `assumed` — derived-on-assumed is reported at the assumption's strength, not the
  numerator's.
- No catalog expectations are declared for this question (none exist in a plan of
  record); the expectations table will be empty rather than invented.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: our NA in-segment units/revenue (measured), an order-of-magnitude
share RANGE (assumed denominator), and the growth-rate comparison vs the assumed market
CAGR. It does NOT assert: a point share, any share-shift vs a named competitor, any
non-US share, or segment sizing beyond A-003's order-of-magnitude confidence. If A-003
is refreshed or replaced by purchased analyst unit data, this answer must be re-run
before reuse.
