## Question

Does the subscription model work at unit level — LTV per connected pump vs cost-to-serve, and is Cloud Suite cannibalizing hardware ASP?

**Asked by**: finance-committee · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The finance committee's quarterly unit-economics read on Cloud Suite: whether a
connected pump is worth serving at current ARR and cost-to-serve, where the model
breaks (site size), and whether the subscription is being bought with hardware
discounts. It equips pricing-floor, minimum-deal-size, and cost-to-serve
interventions.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Population** = subscription register rows joined conceptually to the fleet
  registry (shared site model). **Active sites only** enter the LTV and cost-to-serve
  rates; **churned sites** are excluded from the rates but counted and reported —
  their existence is direct evidence against the no-churn horizon below, and the
  answer must say so rather than bury them.
- **LTV per connected pump (simple undiscounted model — limits stated)** =
  (Σ ARR of active sites ÷ Σ connected pumps at active sites) × `ltv_horizon_years`
  (5). No discounting, no churn decrement, no ARR growth, no expansion revenue. With
  churned sites already on file, the no-churn horizon **overstates** LTV; discounting
  would lower it further. Both stated inline.
- **Cost-to-serve per connected pump** = Σ annual cost_to_serve of active sites ÷
  Σ connected pumps at active sites; over the same horizon for the ratio.
- **LTV : cost-to-serve ratio** = Σ ARR ÷ Σ cost_to_serve over active sites (the
  identical horizon and pump denominator cancel — stated). Guardrail
  `min_ltv_cts_ratio` (3.0) per E-04.1. **Rounding convention (committed)**: every
  ratio, rate, margin, and mean is computed on raw sums/values and rounded only for
  display; guardrail verdicts compare the unrounded value — computing on pre-rounded
  intermediates is the pattern that flips verdicts at thresholds.
- **Site-size split** = bands on `pumps_connected` per `size_bands` in
  `commercial.yml`: small ≤ 6, mid 7–11, large ≥ 12. Per band: sites, pumps, ARR/pump,
  cost/pump, and annual margin/pump (ARR − cost, per pump). The small-site
  negative-margin knob is reported per band, not averaged away.
- **Cannibalization** = simple mean of `hardware_discount_pct` across **active**
  subscribed sites vs `max_asp_erosion_pct` (5) per E-04.2, plus the mean by size
  band. This measures discounting **at subscribed sites**; with no unsubscribed-site
  discount baseline in the corpus, causality (subscription → discount) cannot be
  established and is not claimed. **Threshold sensitivity (committed disclosure)**:
  the E-04.2 verdict rides on a sample mean compared against an unvalidated stand-in
  tolerance, so the answer reports the miss in pp alongside the standard error of the
  mean and flags a knife-edge whenever the miss is within 1pp of the line or within
  ~2 SE of it — a near-threshold not-met is a watch signal, not a breach finding,
  until pricing policy sets a real cap.
- **Fleet context** = connected-pump universe from the fleet registry (connected
  devices; connected sites with no subscription row = the attach gap, counted).
- **History** = the register is current-state (one snapshot). The only derivable
  history is the ARR build from `start_date` of currently-active sites (cumulative
  ARR by start month) — **survivor-biased** (churned sites' past ARR absent) and
  valued at current ARR; charted with that bias stated. True LTV/cost history needs
  recurring snapshots — stated as the gap.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-subscriptions` — per-site ARR, cost to serve, status, hardware
  discount (demo-fabricated; stand-in for the billing/entitlement export).
- `commercial/internal-fleet` — installed-base registry incl. connectivity
  (demo-fabricated; stand-in for the fleet-management export).

Data we have: everything needed for current-state unit economics, the size split, and
the discount signal. Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked): an unsubscribed-
site hardware-discount baseline (to turn the discount signal into a cannibalization
claim); gross-margin on ARR (cost_to_serve is the only cost field; no COGS split);
churned sites' historical ARR (for a real churn-adjusted LTV); recurring snapshots
(for trend).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-04.1** — Lifetime value per connected pump covers cost-to-serve at a healthy
  multiple _(basis: generic SaaS unit-economics heuristic; no underwritten target —
  unvalidated, challengeable)_.
- **E-04.2** — Cloud Suite attach does not erode hardware pricing beyond tolerance
  _(basis: stand-in tolerance; pricing policy sets no explicit cap — unvalidated,
  challengeable)_.
- The LTV model is deliberately the simplest defensible arithmetic; every one of its
  omissions (discounting, churn, growth) is stated with its direction of error.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: per-pump ARR and cost-to-serve rates at active sites, the 5-year
undiscounted LTV under the stated model, the LTV:cost ratio vs the guardrail, the
size-band profitability split, churn and attach-gap counts, and the mean hardware
discount vs tolerance. It does **not** assert: a churn- or discount-adjusted LTV,
causality between subscription and hardware discounts, site-level P&L (allocated
costs beyond cost_to_serve), or a pricing recommendation. Demo-fabricated data —
directional narrative only.
