# Analysis plan — BQ-28

_The analysis contract for this question: what the answer is FOR, how it is computed,
what it assumes, and what it does — and does not — assert. User-owned prose: edit
freely. Editions pin this file's hash; the quality audit calls out drift, and an agent
intent-check verifies the computed answer honors this plan._

## Question

Where are we against plan on revenue — by line and region, volume vs price vs timing?

**Asked by**: cfo · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly reforecast call: whether the CFO holds the FY2026 plan of record, triggers
a reforecast, or reallocates commercial effort between lines and regions. The answer
must separate "total within tolerance" from the line/region mix underneath it — a
composite that hides a flagship miss under a legacy overshoot is the failure mode this
question exists to catch.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Actuals window** = every closed fiscal quarter of the configured `fy` present in the
  pinned `internal-financials` snapshot (currently 2026-Q1 + 2026-Q2 — H1). "YTD" means
  exactly these quarters; no partial-quarter extrapolation.
- **Plan** = the matching quarterly rows of the pinned `internal-revenue-plan` snapshot
  (plan of record LRP-2026.1). Actual vs plan is compared only over matching periods.
- **Variance** = (actual − plan) ÷ plan, as a percentage, computed per product line
  (summed over regions), per region (summed over lines), per quarter (timing), and in
  total. The tolerance for flagging is `tolerance_pct` from `commercial.yml` (±5%,
  itself an unvalidated stand-in — E-28.1).
- **Mix decomposition** = each line's and region's dollar variance contribution to the
  total variance, so the report shows what the composite nets together.
- **Timing** = per-quarter variance across the actuals window (is the miss steady, or
  deteriorating quarter over quarter).
- **Volume vs price split: NOT COMPUTABLE.** The plan of record carries revenue only —
  no planned units. Actuals carry units, but a price/volume bridge needs planned units
  on the other side. The split is published as an `unavailable` series naming exactly
  that missing data; it is never approximated from actual-side ASPs alone.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-financials` (quarterly actuals: revenue, COGS, units)
- `commercial/internal-revenue-plan` (plan of record: revenue by period × line × region)

Data we have: actual revenue/COGS/units by quarter × line × region × revenue type; plan
revenue by quarter × line × region. Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked):
planned units (blocks the volume-vs-price bridge); a plan phasing note for revenue
types (the plan has no revenue_type dimension, so recurring-vs-hardware plan tracking
belongs to BQ-03, not here).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-28.1** — Year-to-date revenue tracks the 2026 plan within tolerance _(basis:
  standard reforecast trigger; the plan of record names no tolerance — unvalidated,
  challengeable)_. Verdict rule: met if |total YTD variance| ≤ tolerance; the same
  tolerance is applied per line and per region as a flagging (not verdict) threshold.
  **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline and the E-28.1 actual both carry the
  compact per-line breach count ("N of M lines breach the band individually") so a
  verdict-only rollup (console badge, expectation chip) cannot drop the line breaches
  the composite nets away (red-team finding F28-1, edition 2026-07-27).
- The plan of record is treated as fixed for the fiscal year (max_age 365d); actuals
  freshness follows the financials dataset's 90d window.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: total, per-line, per-region, and per-quarter variance vs the plan
of record over closed quarters, and which cells breach the (stand-in) tolerance. It
does **not** assert: a volume-vs-price explanation (planned units do not exist), a
full-year forecast (no extrapolation), root cause of any miss (needs win/loss and
funnel data), or a reforecast recommendation — it triggers that conversation, it does
not conclude it.
