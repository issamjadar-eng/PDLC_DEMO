## Question

Where are pumps sold but underused — accounts whose utilization/telemetry diverges from what they bought?

**Asked by**: cco, customer-success · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

Customer-success intervention targeting: which sites are running well below the
utilization their purchase implies (the sold-but-underused early churn warning), which
of those are material (enough connected devices to matter), and what revenue exposure
frames the intervention priority.

## Approach

Committed definitions:

- **Scope**: Cloud Suite CONNECTED devices only — telemetry exists for nothing else.
  Unconnected fleet utilization is unobservable and is published as a stated gap, not
  extrapolated.
- **Window**: trailing `window_months: 3` (the 3 newest months in the pinned telemetry
  snapshot — deterministic against the pin, no wall clock).
- **Site utilization** = Σ `infusion_hours` ÷ Σ `expected_hours` over the window
  (hours-weighted, not an average of monthly ratios). `expected_hours` is the
  dataset's per-segment norm — the "what they bought" proxy.
- **Flagged** = site utilization < `underuse_threshold_pct` (60%). **Material-flagged**
  = flagged AND ≥ `min_devices` (10) connected devices (per the pinned fleet
  registry). Flagged sites below the materiality bar are still listed as a watch tier
  — materiality filters priority, it must not hide the signal.
- **E-11.1 is evaluated at SITE level as a proxy**: the expectation is account-scoped,
  but no pinned dataset carries a site→account key (sales-accounts is account-level
  with no site list), so account-level evaluation is blocked. The proxy verdict and
  the join gap are both stated.
- **Revenue framing**: with the site→account join unavailable, account-level
  revenue-at-risk is published as an `unavailable` series naming the missing key. As
  interim framing, the report gives the REGIONAL direct-book revenue (FY2026H1) for
  each flagged site's region — context, explicitly not attribution.
- **History**: monthly utilization % lines for the three worst flagged sites plus the
  connected-fleet median as reference (4 lines, ≤4 rule; pick rule stated).

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-telemetry-utilization` — monthly infusion-hours vs expected for
  connected devices (2026-02..2026-07, demo-fabricated).
- `commercial/internal-fleet` — per-device registry; connected-device counts per site.
- `commercial/internal-sales-accounts` — account/regional revenue (demo-fabricated).

Data we have: device-month utilization for connected devices, per-site connected
counts, regional revenue. Data we do NOT have: a site→account mapping (blocks the
account-revenue tie the question asks for — flagged as the dataset gap to fix),
unconnected-device utilization, and contracted usage commitments (expected_hours is a
norm, not a contract term).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-11.1** — No material account runs its connected fleet below the utilization
  floor _(basis: stand-in floor; contracts set no usage commitment — unvalidated,
  challengeable; evaluated at site level as a proxy per the join gap above)_.
- `expected_hours` (segment norm) is assumed to represent "what they bought"; a site
  with a legitimately different case mix will look underused against it.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: which connected sites run < 60% of expected hours over the
trailing 3 months, which clear the 10-device materiality bar, and the monthly
trajectory of the worst sites. It does NOT assert: account-level revenue at risk (join
key missing), churn probability, cause of underuse (case mix vs shelfware vs
connectivity gaps), or anything about unconnected devices.
