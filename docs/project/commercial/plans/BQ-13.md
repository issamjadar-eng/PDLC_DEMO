## Question

How much runway before someone closes the predictive-monitoring gap — is our Y3 timing
defensible, and what if an incumbent buys an AI entrant?

**Asked by**: vp-product, board · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly board/product check on the F6 predictive-monitoring bet: does the Y3
(2028-H2) launch slot still hold competitive runway, or does the runway math say an
entrant could clear first — the trigger to accelerate F6, buy instead of build, or
re-frame the differentiation claim. The answer is a runway model laid against observed
public signals, not a prediction of competitor intent.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **"Nobody ships predictive monitoring today" is verified, not asserted**: every
  `predictive_monitoring` row across the pinned competitor-features snapshot must read
  `no …` — if any row reads `yes`, the runway question is moot and the answer says so
  instead of running the model.
- **Data anchor** = the newest `decision_date` in the pinned 510(k) snapshot. All
  runway arithmetic projects forward from this anchor — deterministic against the pin,
  and honest about FDA publication lag (the anchor can trail today).
- **Our launch anchor** = the first day of `our_launch_period` (2028-H2 → 2028-07-01),
  declared in commercial.yml. Using the earliest date we could plausibly launch is the
  *favorable-to-us* reading; if an entrant still beats it, the verdict is robust.
- **Anchor sensitivity is stated, never buried** (red-team RT-13.1): the answer also
  computes the *unfavorable* end-of-period reading (`our_launch_anchor_late`,
  2028-12-31) and states the hardware-scenario fast-edge margin against BOTH readings.
  A "clears after our launch anchor" reassurance that holds by days under one reading
  and flips under the other must say so in the verdict and the runway section, not in
  a footnote. The H2 half-year refinement exists only in the question catalog — the
  strategy doc (commercial-strategy.md D-COMM-1.4) commits F6 to bare "Y3 (2028)";
  the anchor is a config choice lacking strategy-doc grounding, and the answer says so.
- **Runway model** = the A-005 develop-to-clear lead-time bands added to the data
  anchor: SaMD-only entrant ~18–24 months concept→clearance; hardware-integrated /
  clinical-evidence-heavy program ~30–48 months. An entrant "beats us" when its
  projected clearance range closes before our launch anchor.
- **Acquisition scenario** (assumption-class, clearly labeled): an incumbent acquiring
  an AI entrant inherits the SaMD-only clock on an established hospital channel —
  modeled by applying the 18–24-month band to the incumbent, not by any observed
  transaction.
- **Recent-clearance watch** = keyword flags (config `watch_keywords`) on public device
  names in the pinned 510(k) snapshot, applicant names normalized via
  entity-aliases.yml — a triage aid, never a capability judgment.

## Data

Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked): adjacent SaMD/monitoring product codes —

unannounced development programs, which are invisible in public data by construction.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **A-005** carries the entire lead-time model (medium confidence; refresh trigger:
  each 510(k) snapshot refresh, or any predictive-monitoring announcement). The runway
  verdict is assumption-bounded — challenge A-005 itself, not just the arithmetic.
- The entrant clock is modeled as starting no earlier than the data anchor. A program
  already underway shortens every runway figure — the answer must state this
  directionally (entry could be underway undetected), not bury it.
- The launch anchor is a challengeable assumption: `our_launch_period: 2028-H2` has no
  basis outside commercial.yml (the strategy doc commits only "Y3 (2028)"). The main
  SaMD verdict survives every anchor reading (it strengthens under later readings);
  the hardware-scenario posture does NOT — it is anchor-reading-dependent and the
  answer must carry that fragility.
- No catalog expectations are declared for this question.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: that no competitor in the curated set documents shipping
predictive monitoring as of the pins; the modeled clearance windows for a SaMD-only
entrant, a hardware/clinical program, and an incumbent-acquisition scenario, each laid
against our 2028-H2 launch anchor; and which observed clearances flag
software/AI-adjacent keywords. It does **not** assert: that any entrant exists or does
not exist (unannounced programs are invisible), competitor intent or actual timelines,
entrant absence beyond the FRN product-code scope, or a build/buy/accelerate
recommendation — it equips that decision with the runway math and its assumptions.
