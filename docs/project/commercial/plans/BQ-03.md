## Question

What share of revenue is recurring today vs the Y5 plan — and which parts of the $350M target are contracted, modeled, or aspiration?

**Asked by**: board-chair, cfo · **cadence**: annual

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The board's credibility check on the recurring-revenue story: how much of today's
revenue actually recurs, whether the mix is moving the right way, and how much of the
FY2030 target rests on things that exist versus things that must still happen
(regulatory events above all). It equips the "do we believe the Y5 number" discussion.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Recurring (actuals)** = `revenue_type = subscription` in the financial actuals.
  Consumables are NOT counted as recurring here — they re-order, but they are not
  contracted subscription revenue; this is the conservative definition and it is
  stated. Share = subscription revenue ÷ total revenue per fiscal year, for FY2024,
  FY2025, and FY2026H1 (H1 = 2026-Q1..Q2, the periods on file — labeled as H1, never
  annualized).
- **Recurring (plan)** = the plan of record carries no revenue_type, so planned
  recurring revenue is **proxied by the `cloud-suite` product line**
  (`plan_recurring_proxy_line` in `commercial.yml`). The proxy is imperfect in both
  directions (device-line service contracts recur but aren't counted; stated).
  Plan share per year FY2026–FY2030 (FY2026 = sum of quarterly rows).
- **The three-bucket decomposition of the FY2030 target** is defined **against
  `regulatory_dependency`** on the plan rows:
  - **contracted** = `cleared` — revenue from products already cleared today. This is
    an upper bound on truly contracted revenue (cleared ≠ under contract; the plan
    has no contract-term field) — stated.
  - **modeled** = `letter-to-file` — revenue behind planned product changes that need
    only internal documentation, no FDA decision; it requires execution, not
    permission.
  - **aspiration** = `pccp-enabled` + `new-submission` — revenue behind FDA decisions
    **not yet received**. Nothing entitles the company to this revenue today.
  The bucket names appear in headings and chart labels; body rows carry the
  regulatory_dependency values they are defined from.
- **Recurring-specific decomposition (committed)**: the blended decomposition spans
  all six lines, but the question targets recurring revenue — so the same three
  buckets are also computed **within the recurring proxy line alone** and reported
  alongside the blended figures (both stated, neither replacing the other). The
  blended dependent share understates the recurring-specific bet whenever the
  cleared cushion sits mostly in device hardware; the answer must show both.
- The FY2030 plan total is checked against the `y5_target_usd` catalog constant and
  any mismatch is reported, not smoothed over.
- Fiscal-year labels map to ISO dates as YYYY-01-01 for timeseries x-axes; the
  FY2026H1 actual point is plotted at its year with an H1-labeled point.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-financials` — actuals by revenue type (demo-fabricated).
- `commercial/internal-revenue-plan` — plan of record with regulatory_dependency
  tags (demo-fabricated).

Data we have: everything needed for the actual recurring share, the plan trajectory,
and the dependency decomposition. Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked):
contract terms / committed-backlog data (to split "contracted" from "cleared but
uncommitted"); a revenue_type dimension on the plan (to retire the cloud-suite proxy);
and multi-year plan versions (to show how the Y5 target itself has moved).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-03.1** — Recurring (subscription) revenue share grows every fiscal year toward
  the Y5 model _(basis: implied by the razor+recurring strategy (D-COMM); no numeric
  milestone set — unvalidated, challengeable)_. Evaluated on FY2024 → FY2025 →
  FY2026H1 actual shares (strictly increasing); the H1 basis is stated.
- The cloud-suite-as-recurring proxy on the plan side is a definitional choice, not an
  estimate; both directions of its error are stated in the report.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the actual subscription share per fiscal year on file, the
planned recurring-proxy share per plan year, the FY2030 target decomposed into the
three buckets as defined above (blended across all lines AND within the recurring
proxy line alone), and whether E-03.1 holds. It does **not** assert:
that "contracted" revenue is contractually committed (it is cleared-today revenue),
the probability of the aspiration bucket landing (that is BQ-05's exposure/slip
analysis), consumables-inclusive recurring share, or any reforecast of the plan.
Demo-fabricated data — directional narrative only.
