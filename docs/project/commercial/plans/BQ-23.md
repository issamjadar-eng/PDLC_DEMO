## Question

Where do we stand on the current field-update campaign — planned vs actual coverage by
region and site, and will we hit the close date?

**Asked by**: vp-field-service · **cadence**: weekly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The weekly campaign-steering call: hold the wave schedule, add field resources to
lagging regions, or re-baseline the close date and notify accounts. The answer must
separate coverage-to-date (fact) from projected finish (an explicit extrapolation).

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Completed** = status `completed` or `completed-after-retry`; coverage % = completed
  ÷ targeted devices, per region and overall.
- **Run-rate** = completions in the trailing 28-day window ÷ 4, anchored at the latest
  completed_date in the snapshot (the anchor is optimistic when completions have
  stopped — stalls are called out explicitly, never left to the rate to imply).
- **Projected finish** = remaining ÷ run-rate projected from the anchor — valid only
  while the recent rate holds; a region with zero completions in the trailing four
  weeks and work remaining is STALLED and gets an Issue, and its "projection" is not a
  meaningful forecast.
- **Plan references** (close date, coverage target) come from commercial.yml.
- **Historical view** = cumulative coverage % per region, weekly, toward the target.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-upgrade-campaign`

Data we have: per-device campaign telemetry (demo-fabricated stand-in for the
campaign-management export). Data we still need (stated gaps, never faked): the
campaign plan of record — the close date and target are declared config flagged
unvalidated (E-23.1), not a validated baseline.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-23.1 / E-23.2** as declared in commercial.yml — both unvalidated stand-ins.
- The trailing-window rate is assumed representative of near-term throughput; weakest
  for stalled or short-history regions, and the answer must say so where it applies.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: coverage to date, per-region run-rates, projected finishes under
the stated extrapolation, and whether the close-date expectation holds. It does **not**
assert: the cause of a lag or stall, site-level scheduling feasibility, or the
resourcing decision itself. Demo data; not for clinical use.
