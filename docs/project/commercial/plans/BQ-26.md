## Question

Do we have the capacity to execute the remaining waves on schedule — or do we add
headcount, contract labor, or slip?

**Asked by**: vp-field-service · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly hire / contract / slip call: whether field-service leadership adds
headcount or contract labor for the remaining campaign waves, converts on-site work to
remote, or re-baselines the close date with customer notification. The answer must show
the capacity gap per region in units leadership can act on (completions per week), and
must flag stalls as distinct from slow-but-moving regions.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Completed** = status `completed` or `completed-after-retry` in the pinned campaign
  snapshot; **attempted** excludes still-`scheduled` devices.
- **Current rate** = completions in the trailing 28-day window ÷ 4, anchored at the
  latest completed_date in the snapshot. This anchor is optimistic when completions have
  stopped (zero-days after the last completion are excluded); a stall must therefore be
  called out explicitly, not left to the rate to imply.
- **Required rate** = remaining devices ÷ weeks from the window anchor to the campaign
  close date declared in `commercial.yml`.
- A region with **zero completions in the trailing four weeks and work remaining is a
  stalled region** — reported as a materialized Issue, not a Risk, regardless of what
  the trailing-window arithmetic says its "rate" is.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-upgrade-campaign`

Data we have: per-device campaign telemetry (status, dates, method, region). Data we
still need (stated gaps, never faked): the FSE roster/utilization dataset — until it
exists, capacity conclusions are run-rate projections and the answer must say so; a
device-connectivity field — until it exists, remote-conversion mitigations are unsized.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-26.1** — Existing field-service capacity absorbs the remaining waves without
  surge or slip _(basis: implicit in the wave plan; never validated against an FSE
  roster — unvalidated, challengeable)_.
- The trailing-window run-rate is assumed representative of near-term capacity; this is
  weakest for regions with short history or recent stalls, and the answer must not
  present projections from stalled regions as meaningful forecasts.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the per-region capacity gap (current vs required completions/week),
which regions are stalled vs slow, and whether E-26.1 holds. It does **not** assert:
the cause of a stall (scheduling vs technical — needs field input), the cost of any
mitigation (needs the FSE roster and a connectivity field), or a recommendation between
hire / contract / slip — it equips that decision, it does not make it.
