## Question

Where exactly do we sit on PCA feature parity, line by line — which gaps are on the
roadmap vs silently unaddressed?

**Asked by**: vp-product · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly roadmap-council parity review: for every attribute in the curated
competitive matrix, are we ahead, at parity, or behind — and for every "behind", is a
roadmap lane already committed to close it (F-lane + year) or is the gap **silently
unaddressed** (no lane owns it)? Silently-unaddressed gaps are the headline finding
class: they are the ones nobody has decided about.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Matrix** = the pinned `external-competitor-features` snapshot; our rows are the
  configured `our_vendor` (GlobalLogic / PP3500); everything else is the competitive set.
- **Attribute typing**: a value starting `yes` / `no` / `planned` is capability-typed;
  otherwise numeric-typed with a direction from config `numeric_directions`
  (flow accuracy lower-is-better, battery higher, weight lower). A numeric range
  `a-b` scores as the end most favorable to the competitor (conservative against us).
- **Our status per attribute**: `yes` = shipping; `planned` = roadmap-committed, not
  shipping today; `no` = lacking; **no row at all = undocumented** — treated as *not
  shipping* (absence of evidence scored conservatively, and labeled `undocumented` so
  the reader sees the difference).
- **Verdict per attribute**: `behind` when at least one competitor ships and we do not
  (or the best competitor value is strictly better than ours); `ahead` when we ship /
  are strictly better and no competitor matches; `parity` otherwise.
- **Routing of "behind"**: attribute mapped in config `attribute_lane_map` → *gap on
  roadmap (F-N, year per `lane_years`)*; unmapped → **SILENTLY UNADDRESSED**.
- **Lane relation — closure vs adjacency** (red-team RT-14.1): a lane assignment does
  not imply the lane closes the gap. Config `attribute_lane_relation` labels each
  mapping `closes` (the lane ships the missing capability itself) or `adjacent` (the
  nearest roadmap lane responds via a *different mechanism* — e.g. F4 smart alarm
  filtering vs BD's hardware PCA Pause, F6 predictive monitoring vs an integrated
  EtCO2 module). Adjacency-routed gaps are labeled as such in the matrix, the
  headline, and the narrative — "gap on roadmap" for them means "gap acknowledged",
  not "gap being closed", and the competitor's shipped capability may remain
  unanswered after the lane lands.
- **Provisional cells**: any comparison touching a `verified: verify` row is flagged
  provisional — the verdict stands but must be re-confirmed before external use.
- An attribute with **zero rows for every vendor** (e.g. dose_personalization) is a
  no-data attribute — reported as a stated matrix gap, never scored.

## Assumptions & expectations

- The curated matrix is assumed representative of the shipping competitive set at the
  snapshot date; a capability a vendor ships but has not documented publicly would make
  a "behind" verdict too generous to us, and an `undocumented` on our side may
  understate us — both directions are stated.
- The lane map and lane years are configuration (from commercial-strategy.md
  D-COMM-1.4), cited as config — a strategy re-sequencing must update them.
- No catalog expectations are declared for this question.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the line-by-line parity verdicts, which "behind" gaps have a
committed roadmap lane (with year and closure-vs-adjacency relation), which are
silently unaddressed, and which verdicts are provisional pending source verification.
It does **not** assert: that an adjacency-routed lane closes its gap (it does not —
stated per gap), competitor capability beyond documented rows, feature quality or
clinical merit (a `yes` is binary), the correctness of roadmap sequencing (that is
BQ-17's composite), or any market-share consequence of a gap.
