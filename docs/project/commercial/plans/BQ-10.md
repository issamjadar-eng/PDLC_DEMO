## Question

Where do we sit vs Alaris/Spectrum IQ/Plum 360 on 5-year TCO per pump — the buyer's spreadsheet?

**Asked by**: cco, cfo · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

Pricing-desk and deal-support positioning: what a hospital buyer's 5-year
total-cost-of-ownership spreadsheet plausibly shows for us vs the three incumbent LVP
platforms, and how confident we are allowed to be about it. Competitor realized pricing
is confidential (GPO/IDN contracts), so the competitor side is an assumption-bounded
RANGE (A-004) — the verdict must stay "indicative, assumption-bounded", never a hard
we-win claim.

## Approach

Committed definitions:

- **Our 5-yr TCO (full basis)** = `pp3500_capital_usd` + 5 × (`cloud_suite_per_pump_annual_usd`
  + `consumables_per_pump_annual_usd` + `service_per_pump_annual_usd`) — all four
  constants from `commercial.yml` params, cited `[config: commercial.yml]`. These are
  demo stand-ins for finance-validated rates.
- **Comparable basis (capital + service only)**: A-004's competitor range deliberately
  EXCLUDES consumables (undisclosed) and quantifies software subscription only as
  "required, magnitude unknown". The like-for-like comparison therefore uses our
  capital + 5-yr service against A-004's capital + service range — and states that the
  competitor's true full TCO is strictly HIGHER than its quoted range (their excluded
  consumables + software are positive costs, and networked-integration capital roughly
  doubles standalone capital per A-004).
- **Per-competitor rows**: A-004 does not split pricing by vendor, so Alaris /
  Spectrum IQ / Plum 360 share one class-wide range; what differs per competitor is
  the FEATURE context from the curated matrix (DERS, wireless, predictive monitoring,
  battery, accuracy), with each cell's `verified: yes|verify` status carried — a
  `verify` cell is a named-but-unfetched source, not settled fact.
- **History**: none exists (single A-004 vintage, single curated-matrix snapshot) —
  published as an `unavailable` timeseries naming what would build it (dated A-004
  refreshes on procurement disclosures), never a faked trend.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/external-competitor-features` — curated real public + project-doc
  feature/spec matrix (source per row; `verified` flag per row).

Data we have: our own cost constants (config stand-ins), competitor feature cells with
per-row sources, A-004's compiled public pricing anchors. Data we do NOT have:
competitor realized (transacted) prices, competitor consumables pricing, competitor
software-subscription magnitude — all absorbed into A-004's stated range or excluded
with the exclusion restated in the report.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **A-004** — competitor realized pricing / 5-yr TCO inputs (confidence: LOW;
  consumables excluded, software subscription unquantified). Every competitor dollar
  figure carries `[assume: A-004]`.
- Our own constants are config stand-ins, not finance-validated — flagged in the
  narrative as a validation gap.
- No catalog expectations are declared for this question; the expectations table will
  be empty rather than invented.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: our 5-yr TCO under the declared constants, the comparable-basis
position vs A-004's class-wide range, and per-competitor feature context with
verification status. It does NOT assert: that we beat any named competitor on TCO (the
ranges overlap and the competitor floor reflects refurb/low anchors), a deal-level
price position (realized prices are negotiated), or consumables economics on either
side of the comparison. Verdict language is capped at "indicative, assumption-bounded".
