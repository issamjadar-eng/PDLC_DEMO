## Question

Is the Cloud Suite attach-rate stage-gate holding — the gate that releases the $24M predictive-monitoring spend?

**Asked by**: cfo, board, cco · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The stage-gate decision itself: whether the board/CFO releases the $24M
predictive-monitoring development spend on the strength of Cloud Suite traction. The
answer must give the gate metric under a precisely stated definition, show the go-get
gap (where attach could be won next), and say plainly that the gate NUMBER is a
stand-in — the strategy of record gates the spend on "traction" without defining it.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Attach rate (the gate metric)** = pumps under an ACTIVE Cloud Suite subscription ÷
  PP3500 installed base. Numerator = sum of `pumps_connected` over rows with
  `status = active` in the pinned subscription register. Denominator = count of PP3500
  devices in the pinned fleet registry (the project's only sanctioned installed-base
  denominator). Churned subscriptions count zero — attach means paying today, not ever
  attached. PP3000 is excluded from the denominator: it has no connected devices and
  Cloud Suite is sold on the PP3500 base.
- **Denominator sensitivity (committed disclosure — red-team finding F29-1, edition
  2026-07-27)**: the report prints the attach reading under the whole-PCA denominator
  (PP3500 + PP3000) alongside the primary PP3500-only basis — as a table and a derived
  series, with a verdict clause — because the denominator choice alone can move the
  reading across the gate. The basis defense (zero connected PP3000, no PP3000 adapter
  path in the corpus, Cloud Suite sells on the PP3500 base) is stated with the
  sensitivity, never in place of it: with the gate number unratified, the metric
  definition is equally unratified, and both must be ratified together.
- **Connected-but-unsubscribed (the go-get gap)** = connected PP3500 devices at sites
  with no active subscription row — the technically-ready, commercially-unconverted
  base. This includes both the seeded attach-gap sites and churned sites; site-level
  detail is listed so commercial can act on it.
- **Not connected** = the remainder of the PP3500 base — reachable only through the
  connectivity/adapter path (BQ-30), not through sales motion alone.
- **Verdict rule** = attach ≥ `gate_attach_pct` (from `commercial.yml`) → gate holds at
  the stand-in level; below → not holding. In BOTH cases the report states that the
  gate number is not of record: commercial-strategy.md conditions the $24M on "Y1/Y2
  Cloud Suite traction" with no numeric threshold, so E-29.1 is unvalidated and the
  gate verdict is only as strong as a number nobody has ratified.
- **History** = cumulative pumps under currently-active subscriptions by subscription
  start month (measured). A true attach-% history needs historical fleet snapshots and
  churn dates — neither exists yet, so the attach-% trend is published as an
  `unavailable` series naming that gap, not faked from today's denominator.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/internal-subscriptions` (per-site register: pumps_connected, status, start_date)
- `commercial/internal-fleet` (per-device registry: model, site, region, connected)

Data we have: current-state subscription register and fleet registry that reconcile by
construction (pumps_connected equals the fleet's connected count per site). Data we
still need (stated gaps, never faked): a ratified gate threshold in the strategy or
plan of record; churn end-dates (blocks historical attach); historical fleet snapshots
(blocks the attach-% trend).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-29.1** — Installed-base Cloud Suite attach reaches the stage-gate level before
  the predictive-monitoring spend releases _(basis: numeric stand-in —
  commercial-strategy.md gates the $24M on "Y1/Y2 Cloud Suite traction" but sets NO
  number [VERIFY] — unvalidated, challengeable)_.
- The subscription register and fleet snapshot are treated as the same point in time;
  their snapshot dates differ by days, within both datasets' freshness windows.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: the attach rate under the stated definition, the verdict against
the stand-in gate, the size and location of the connected-but-unsubscribed go-get gap,
and per-region attach. It does **not** assert: that the gate threshold is the right
number (nobody has set one — that is the finding), attach momentum (history is a
stated gap), subscription unit economics (BQ-04), or a release/hold recommendation on
the $24M — it gives the gate reading, not the investment decision.
