## Question

What did competitors clear in the last 90 days, laid against our Y1–Y5 roadmap —
anything landing on a feature we scheduled two years out?

**Asked by**: vp-product · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly roadmap-council triage: does any new public clearance land on a roadmap
lane we have scheduled later — the trigger to accelerate, re-sequence, or reframe a
differentiation claim. The answer is a triage list, not a verdict on competitive
capability.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Sweep window** = the 90 days ending at the NEWEST decision_date in the pinned
  snapshot — deterministic against the pin. Because FDA publication lags, the window's
  end can trail today's date; the window end is always stated so the reader sees the
  lag rather than assuming currency.
- **Roadmap flags** = keyword matches on the public device name against the lane map
  declared in commercial.yml — a triage aid for analyst review, never a capability
  judgment.
- **Historical view** = clearances per quarter across the full snapshot.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `commercial/openfda-510k-infusion`

Data we have: public FRN clearance records. Data we still need (stated gaps, never
faked): adjacent SaMD/monitoring product codes — FRN alone cannot support a
"no entrant activity" conclusion; a wider corpus dataset is required before that claim.

## Assumptions & expectations

- Device names are assumed informative enough for keyword triage; a cleared feature may
  hide behind an uninformative name — the flags under-catch, they never over-assert.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: which FRN clearances occurred in the stated window and which match
roadmap-lane keywords for review. It does **not** assert: competitor capability or
intent, roadmap impact (that is the council's judgment), or entrant absence beyond the
FRN scope.
