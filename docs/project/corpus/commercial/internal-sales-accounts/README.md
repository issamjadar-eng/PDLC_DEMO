# Corpus dataset — `commercial/internal-sales-accounts`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** account-level revenue + installed units for FY2024, FY2025, and
FY2026H1 (554 rows; 39 accounts — 33 customers + 6 prospects). Generated by the seeded
`gen.py` (seed 42) — the stand-in for a CRM / sales-ops revenue-by-account export.
Accounts map onto the 48-site fleet model (identical embedded `make_fleet`, same seed
as `internal-fleet`): single sites or small systems grouping 2–3 sites; prospect
accounts (`ACC-Pnn`) carry **zero installed units**. All health-system and GPO names
are **fictional**.

**Scope note / reconciliation**: account rows carry the **direct book only —
hardware + subscription revenue**. Consumables and service flow through distributors
and are not account-attributed here. Per-FY account revenue reconciles with
`internal-financials` hardware+subscription revenue (same embedded financial model;
only that dataset's ±4% cell noise separates them) — measured at this snapshot:
FY2024 $32.5M vs $32.6M (−0.4%), FY2025 $40.7M vs $40.6M (+0.1%), FY2026H1 $25.1M vs
$24.9M (+0.9%) — all inside the ±5% contract.

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list):

- Concentration (BQ-02): top GPO "Meridian Health Alliance" holds the biggest NA
  accounts — **38.4% of FY2025 revenue**; three smaller fictional GPOs (Cascadia
  Health Partners, AtlasPoint Purchasing, NovaBridge Alliance) + `independent`
  (all EMEA/APAC accounts buy independent).
- Top-3 accounts are noticeably large: 25.5% of FY2025 revenue (largest,
  Northgate Health System, 11.0%).
- PP3500/PP3000/cloud-suite units come from the shared fleet model (686 / 198 / 331
  connected at FY2026H1); IP5000/SP units are bed-derived. Installed-base ramps per
  FY mirror the portfolio story (PP3500 ramping, legacy shrinking, cloud attach
  accelerating).
- Segments: idn (multi-site systems) | academic | community | home-infusion.
  No clocks — literal FY labels, as-of 2026-07-25 (FY2026H1 through 2026-06-30).

**Consumed by**: BQ-02 (customer/GPO concentration), BQ-07 (segment mix), BQ-11
(adoption divergence, joined with `internal-fleet` via shared sites).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs.
- The embedded `make_fleet` and FINANCIAL MODEL blocks must stay byte-identical with
  `internal-fleet/gen.py` and `internal-financials/gen.py` respectively — that identity
  is what keeps sites and revenue coherent across datasets.
