# Corpus dataset — `commercial/internal-financials`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** quarterly financial actuals, 2024-Q1 through 2026-Q2 (480 rows:
period × product line × region × revenue type). Generated by the seeded `gen.py`
(seed 42) — the stand-in for an ERP / financial-reporting export in a real deployment.
Product lines are the five device lines (IP5000, PP3000, PP3500, SP6000, SP6500) plus
`cloud-suite` (subscription revenue only); revenue types are
hardware | consumables | service | subscription.

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — these are modeled, not observed):

- FY2025 totals ~$80.5M with subscription ~7.9% (~8% recurring anchor).
- PP3500 flagship ramps ~10%/quarter since 2024 (FY2025 $34M) — the growth that funds
  the company; `cloud-suite` is small but accelerating (~16%/quarter, FY2025 $6.4M).
- PP3000/IP5000 legacy lines decline ~3–4%/quarter with thinning hardware margins
  (COGS ratio climbs ~1.2 pts/quarter); SP6000/SP6500 steady.
- FY2026 H1 actuals ~$46.3M — annualizing below the $105M plan of record
  (`internal-revenue-plan`), i.e. the plan is deliberately a stretch.
- Deterministic trend model + ±4% per-cell uniform noise; no clocks — literal periods,
  as-of 2026-07-25. The trend model (anchors, growth rates, revenue-type splits) is
  embedded identically in `internal-sales-accounts/gen.py` so account revenue
  reconciles with these actuals.

**Consumed by**: BQ-01 (revenue composition), BQ-03 (plan vs actuals, joined with
`internal-revenue-plan`), BQ-28 (recurring-revenue trajectory).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs.
