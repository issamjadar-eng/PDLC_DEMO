# Corpus dataset — `commercial/internal-regulatory-docket`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** regulatory docket: MDR + field-action log, 2025-01 → 2026-07
(33 records: 30 MDRs, 2 corrections, 1 FSCA, 0 removals). Generated by seeded `gen.py`
(seed 42, no clocks; data through 2026-07-25) — stand-in for the regulatory-affairs
tracking system export. Each record carries its regulatory deadline, filing date (empty
if not yet filed), and status.

**Consumed by**: BQ-21 (MDR filing posture — on-time rate, open items vs deadline,
field-action rollout); BQ-20 (derived complaint↔docket watch-category linkage —
category-level join, no shared key).

## Deterministic knobs (planted narrative beats)

- **Exactly 2 late-filed MDRs** (`filed_date` > `regulatory_deadline`):
  `MDR-2025-0007` (4 days late) and `MDR-2025-0018` (9 days late).
- **1 MDR currently open near its deadline**: `MDR-2026-0005` (over-delivery, opened
  2026-06-29, deadline 2026-07-29 — 4 days out at the 2026-07-25 data horizon). Ties to
  the over-delivery complaint beat in `internal-complaints` (BQ-20 signal watch).
- **1 open FSCA**: `FSCA-2026-001` — drug-library correction (v3.4.1 hard-limit update),
  opened 2026-05-12, report filed on time; region rollout status in the description
  (NA complete, EMEA ~60%, APAC pending).
- **0 removals** — the field-action log holds corrections only.
- All other MDRs are filed on time (3–12 days before deadline); pre-2026 items closed,
  2026 items filed.

## Known cross-dataset seam (intentional)

The docket carries **no 2025 over-delivery MDR**, yet `internal-complaints` record
`C-2025-0431` (over-delivery, 2025-08-14) is flagged `mdr_filed: yes`. This is
**deliberate demo narrative, not a data bug**: it enacts the under-docketing blind spot
BQ-21's risk narrative points at — the docket audits filing timeliness of what was
docketed, and a complaint whose MDR never reached the docket is invisible here. Do not
regenerate either dataset to close the gap.

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs. Generator is deterministic
  (seed 42, fixed `DATA_THROUGH` horizon, no clocks).
