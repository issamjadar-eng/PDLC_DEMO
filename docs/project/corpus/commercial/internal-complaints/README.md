# Corpus dataset — `commercial/internal-complaints`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** 18-month complaint log (435 records: category, severity 1–3, device
linkage, MDR-filed flag). Generated by seeded `gen.py` — stand-in for the
complaint-handling system export. Narrative: battery complaints skew PP3000/older
firmware; volumes trend upward over the window.

**Consumed by**: BQ-18 (trends vs thresholds), BQ-20 (signal watch). (BQ-21 reads the
regulatory docket only and BQ-22 the signal register only — earlier listing here was
overbroad; `commercial.yml` `corpus_deps` is the authoritative consumer wiring.)

## Rare high-severity categories (BQ-20 franchise-killer signal watch)

Two rare categories were added (2026-07-27) on top of the stable volume categories —
the low-count, high-consequence signals BQ-20 watches for. For a PCA pump, over-delivery
and PCA-by-proxy are the franchise-killer failure modes: single events, not trends, are
the signal. Generated from a separate rng stream (seed+2), so the original 430 records
are unchanged; these 5 carry `severity: 3` — the dataset's single severity convention
(3 = severe; MDR filing only occurs at severity 3). An earlier same-day snapshot briefly
carried them at severity 1 under an inverted convention; corrected 2026-07-27 so
consumers can filter "severe" uniformly:

- `over-delivery` (3): `C-2025-0431` (MDR filed), `C-2026-0432`, `C-2026-0433`
  (MDR filed, under investigation — ties to `internal-regulatory-docket` `MDR-2026-0005`).
- `pca-by-proxy-suspected` (2): `C-2025-0434`, `C-2026-0435` (suspected unauthorized
  bolus by family/visitor; no MDR filed).

### Known cross-dataset seam (intentional)

`C-2025-0431` (over-delivery, 2025-08-14) carries `mdr_filed: yes`, but
`internal-regulatory-docket` holds **no 2025 over-delivery MDR** — its only
over-delivery item is `MDR-2026-0005` (matching `C-2026-0433`). This gap is
**deliberate demo narrative, not a data bug**: an MDR-flagged complaint absent from the
docket illustrates exactly the under-docketing blind spot BQ-21's risk narrative warns
about (the docket audits what was docketed, not whether everything reportable was
docketed). Do not "fix" either generator to close the gap.

## Conventions

  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
