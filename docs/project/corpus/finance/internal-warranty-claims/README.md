# Corpus dataset — `finance/internal-warranty-claims`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** per-claim warranty log, 2025-01 through 2026-08 (~600 claims).
Generated by the seeded `gen.py` (seed 42) — the stand-in for the service-management /
warranty-claims system export in a real deployment. Each claim carries product line
(the five device lines of `commercial/internal-financials`), region (NA / EMEA / APAC),
hardware revision as the **service system** records it, claim date, failure category,
cost, disposition (repair | replace | goodwill | denied) and a `linked_complaint` flag
(whether the claim was tied to a record in the complaint-handling system).

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — modeled, not observed):

- **PP3000 claims cluster on hw rev B** (~70% of PP3000 claims), with pump-mechanism and
  power-supply failures dominating that cluster.
- **Warranty cost per unit is rising for legacy lines**: PP3000 monthly claim volume
  roughly doubles across the window and its replace share climbs from ~25% to ~40%;
  IP5000 drifts up more gently. PP3500 is steady; SP6000 / SP6500 low and flat.
- ~33% of claims link to a complaint (~45% inside the PP3000 rev-B cluster).
- Replacement costs sit near each line's hardware unit cost in the financials.
- No clocks — literal dates; as-of 2026-08-31.
- Both knobs are **proven at acquire/validate** by `check_knobs.py` (rev-B share ≥ 60%;
  PP3000 last-6-month cost ≥ 1.3× first-6-month cost).

**Denominator caveat for consumers**: the fleet registry (`commercial/internal-fleet`)
records PP3000 `hw_rev` as `-` and carries no IP5000 / SP6000 / SP6500 devices. Warranty
cost **per installed unit** is therefore computable at line level for PP3500 (by rev A / B)
and PP3000 (line level only); per-rev for PP3000 and per-unit for the other three lines
are stated gaps, never approximated.

**Consumed by**: FQ-03 (cost of poor quality); roadmap FQ-04 (warranty & service reserve
adequacy vs field failure rate).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs.
