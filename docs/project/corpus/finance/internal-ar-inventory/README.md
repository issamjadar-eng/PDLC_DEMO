# Corpus dataset — `finance/internal-ar-inventory`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** monthly working-capital cube, 2025-01 through 2026-08 (1,200 rows:
period × region × channel × product line). Generated by the seeded `gen.py` (seed 42) —
the stand-in for the ERP AR-aging + inventory-valuation export in a real deployment.
Channels are three **fictional** GPOs (Meridian Health Alliance, Northgate Purchasing
Group, Cascadia Supply Co-op) plus `direct`; product lines are the five device lines of
`commercial/internal-financials` (cloud-suite subscription billing carries no inventory
and is out of scope — a stated gap). Per row: `ar_balance_usd`, `ar_over_90_usd` (aged
> 90 days), `dso_days`, `inventory_usd` + `inventory_days` (finished goods held against
that channel's demand, in days of cost consumption), `consumable_units_shipped`.

**Aggregation convention for consumers** (ratios do not sum): DSO over any slice =
Σ AR ÷ Σ (AR ÷ dso_days); inventory days = Σ inventory ÷ Σ (inventory ÷ inventory_days);
over-90 share = Σ over-90 ÷ Σ AR.

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — modeled, not observed):

- **Northgate Purchasing Group's over-90 AR is climbing**: over-90 share ~8% → ~32% and
  DSO ~48 → ~78 days across 2026 (a collections dispute building). The other channels
  hold DSO in the mid-40s / low-50s; EMEA / APAC carry +6 / +10 days structurally.
- **Inventory days rising on legacy hardware**: PP3000 ~72 → ~135 days and IP5000 ~68 →
  ~120 days across the window; PP3500 steady ~55; SP6000 / SP6500 ~62 flat.
- Revenue basis = the `internal-financials` FINANCIAL MODEL (copied block — keep in
  sync), so AR is DSO-consistent with the ~$80.5M FY2025 anchor; ±3% per-cell noise.
- No clocks — literal periods; as-of 2026-08-31.
- Both knobs are **proven at acquire/validate** by `check_knobs.py` (Northgate over-90
  share at least doubles; PP3000 days +30 while PP3500 holds within ±10).

**Consumed by**: FQ-06 (working capital — DSO, over-90 AR, inventory days); roadmap FQ-07
(consumable pull-through vs installed base).
