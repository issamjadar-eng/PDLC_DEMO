# Corpus dataset — `finance/internal-gl-budget`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** monthly GL actual-vs-budget, 2026-01 through 2026-08 (120 rows:
period × 15 cost centers across six functions — R&D, Regulatory/Quality, Manufacturing,
Sales & Marketing, G&A, Service), each with budgeted and actual spend and headcount.
Generated by the seeded `gen.py` (seed 42) — the stand-in for the general-ledger / FP&A
budget-vs-actual export in a real deployment. The operating-expense envelope
(~$3.55M / month) is sized against the `commercial/internal-financials` FY2025 revenue
anchor (~$80.5M).

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — modeled, not observed):

- **R&D over budget** from 2026-03: the PP3500 firmware 3.4 campaign lands on cost center
  `RD-FW` (contractors + test rigs, +3 contract heads); `RD-SW` / `RD-HW` near plan.
- **Regulatory/Quality over budget** on external consulting: `RQ-EXT` runs ~1.6–2.0× its
  budget (submission support); `RQ-REG` / `RQ-QA` near plan.
- **Sales & Marketing under budget** — hiring lag: the `SM-NA` / `SM-INTL` headcount plan
  ramps +1 head/month while actual heads lag by 3–5; spend follows (~0.88×).
- Manufacturing, G&A, Service inside ±3%; ±2% per-cell noise.
- No clocks — literal periods; as-of 2026-08-31.
- The knobs are **proven at acquire/validate** by `check_knobs.py` (R&D and
  Regulatory/Quality > +5% YTD, Sales & Marketing < −5% with a headcount shortfall, the
  rest inside ±5%).

**Consumed by**: FQ-08 (budget vs actual YTD by function with headcount variance);
roadmap FQ-10 (R&D spend vs milestone — capitalization view).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs.
