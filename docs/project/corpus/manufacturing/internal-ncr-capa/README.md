# Corpus dataset — `manufacturing/internal-ncr-capa`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** nonconformance (NCR) + CAPA register, 2025-01 through 2026-08
(286 records: 207 NCRs, 79 CAPAs; status as of 2026-08-31). Generated by the seeded
`gen.py` (seed 42, no clocks) — the stand-in for an eQMS NCR/CAPA module export. Every
`status` is **derived from the record's own dates** against the data horizon (closed →
`closed`; due date passed → `overdue-open`; opened in the last two weeks → `open`;
otherwise `in-progress`), so consumers can rebuild the open / past-due population at any
month-end from `opened_date` / `due_date` / `closed_date` alone. NCRs are due 30 days
after opening, CAPAs 90 days. `supplier_id` (supplier-sourced records) reuses the
`manufacturing/internal-suppliers` ids; `linked_record` points an NCR at the CAPA it
escalated to.

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — modeled, not observed; asserted by
`check_knobs.py`):

- **CAPA aging past due rises**: closure probability falls by open cohort (95% before
  2025-10 → 60% → 25% from 2026-03), so past-due open CAPAs climb from 3 at 2025-12-31
  to 21 at 2026-08-31, including critical ones.
- **Supplier-sourced NCRs concentrate on SUP-003** (the single-source pump-motor
  supplier): 21 of 45 supplier NCRs, heavier in 2026.
- **Effectiveness verification lags**: verified share of closed CAPAs falls by closure
  cohort (~76% for 2025 closures → ~15% for 2026 closures).

**Consumed by**: MQ-03 (NCR rate and aging, CAPA open / past-due share, effectiveness
backlog by source and severity).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs. Generator is deterministic
  (seed 42, fixed `DATA_THROUGH` horizon, no clocks); `data_through: 2026-08-31` lands
  in provenance as `as_of`.
- `effectiveness_verified` is populated for CAPAs only (`true` / `false`); NCR rows leave
  it empty. `product_line` `shared` = site / QMS-level records.
