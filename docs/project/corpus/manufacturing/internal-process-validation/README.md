# Corpus dataset — `manufacturing/internal-process-validation`

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

**Demo-fabricated** validation + calibration status register (155 records; status as of
2026-08-31). Generated by the seeded `gen.py` (seed 42, no clocks) — the stand-in for
the validation master list (IQ / OQ / PQ per equipment or process) joined to the
calibration and preventive-maintenance register. Composition: 67 IQ/OQ/PQ records over
20 established subjects + the PP3500 rev C items, 68 calibrations (34 per site,
12-month interval), 20 preventive-maintenance records (6-month interval). `product_line`
is empty for shared equipment. Every `status` is **derived from the record's own dates**
against the horizon: one-shot validations (IQ/OQ/PQ) are `validated` once completed,
`overdue` if the planned date passed without completion, else `in-progress` /
`not-started`; recurring items (calibration / PM) are `overdue` if the next due date
passed, `due` within 30 days of the horizon, else `validated`.

**Narrative knobs seeded** (honest list — modeled, not observed; asserted by
`check_knobs.py`):

  in-progress at both sites, due 2026-09-30** — plus a rev C occlusion-test process OQ at
  Westfield not started (due 2026-10-15). These are the validation-side blockers MQ-08
  sets against the rev C lots already released in `internal-production-lots`.
- **Cluster of overdue calibrations at Westfield** (9, up to ~120 days past due);
  Eastbrook has 1.
- A handful of preventive-maintenance items fall due within 30 days of the horizon.

**Consumed by**: MQ-08 (validation and calibration posture; PP3500 rev C release
blockers, joined with `internal-production-lots`); roadmap MQ-10 (design-transfer
readiness).

## Conventions

- Managed by the `corpus` skill (`corpus.py`). Snapshots under `snapshots/` are immutable —
  never hand-edit; re-acquire instead. `dataset.yml` is the config; `latest` points at the
  newest valid snapshot. Assumptions in `assumptions/A-NNN.yml`, waivers in `waivers/W-NNN.yml`.
- Regenerate via `corpus.py acquire` — never edit CSVs. Generator is deterministic
  (seed 42, fixed `DATA_THROUGH` horizon, no clocks); `data_through: 2026-08-31` lands
  in provenance as `as_of`.
- A single snapshot is a point-in-time posture; a posture *trend* needs recurring
  snapshots (`max_age_days: 30` cadence) — consumers publish the trend as unavailable
  until then.
