## 1. Purpose 📤

Proposed device-level IFU for the PP3500 PCA infusion pump, with the care setting, patient population, and intended users.

## 2. Proposed IFU — Device 📤

> _"The PainEase PCA Advanced (PP3500) is intended for the controlled intravenous, subcutaneous, or epidural administration of analgesic medications to adult and pediatric patients in professional healthcare settings. The device supports clinician-programmed continuous infusion and patient-controlled demand dosing within clinician-configured dose and time limits enforced by an on-device drug library."_

This is the sponsor's proposed wording. The predicate's cleared statement (PP3000, K190567) is the reference point; the differences — administration routes, pediatric population floor, continuous-infusion mode, and the on-device drug library — are addressed in the substantial-equivalence discussion.

## 3. Patient Population 📤

Adults and pediatric patients ≥ 6 years and ≥ 20 kg requiring analgesic infusion under clinician supervision. This lowers the predicate's pediatric floor (≥ 8 years, ≥ 25 kg); the substantial-equivalence discussion addresses whether the expanded population raises different questions of safety or effectiveness.

## 4. Care Setting 📤

Professional healthcare facilities (hospital, surgical center). Home use is out of scope for this filing.

## 5. Intended Users 📤

Licensed clinicians program and manage therapy; patients self-administer demand doses within configured limits.

## 6. Contraindications 📤

Contraindications carry over from the predicate labeling with two changes: the pediatric exclusion is lowered to < 6 years or < 20 kg, and paralytic ileus is added.

## 7. Clinical Context 📖

_Reference — acute and post-operative pain management; analgesic classes per the configured drug library._
