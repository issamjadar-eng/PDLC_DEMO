# Drug-Library-Manager Accessory Brief — PP3500 Q-Sub

_Demo sample data — not for clinical use._

> **🔒 INTERNAL.** Strengthener brief (strongly-desired, not transmission-blocking) anchoring Q1.1. Status draft-v0.0 — stub for the demo.

## 1. Position 📤

The Drug Library Manager is a Class II SaMD accessory: it authors the dose-limit table the PP3500 enforces. Because its safety role is inseparable from the pump's dose enforcement, we propose clearing it as an accessory bundled into the PP3500 510(k).

## 2. Bundle vs Standalone 📤

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Bundle (proposed) | Single review of the coupled dose-enforcement chain; one PCCP | Larger filing |
| Standalone | Independent lifecycle | Splits the dose-enforcement safety argument across two filings |
