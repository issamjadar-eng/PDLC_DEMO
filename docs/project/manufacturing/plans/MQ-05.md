## Question

Supplier quality — incoming reject rate and on-time by supplier and component family, scorecard distribution, single-source exposure, audit currency.

**Asked by**: supplier-quality, vp-quality · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly supplier-quality review: which suppliers get a supplier corrective action
request or tightened incoming sampling, which audits must be scheduled now, and whether
a single-source exposure warrants second-source qualification or safety stock. The
answer must keep a healthy supply-base average from hiding one failing single-source
supplier — the case where the plant has no fallback.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Window** = the trailing `window_months` periods (catalog `params`, 3) ending with the
  month of the pinned snapshot's provenance `as_of`, restricted to periods present in
  the pin.
- **Reject rate** = Σ `lots_rejected` ÷ Σ `lots_received` over the window, per supplier,
  per component family, and overall — **lot-weighted**. **On-time** = lot-weighted mean of
  the period `on_time_pct`.
- **Above ceiling** = window reject rate > `reject_ceiling_pct`; compared unrounded.
- **Register fields** (name, family, single-source flag, approval status, audit dates,
  scorecard) are read from each supplier's **latest-period row**; the scorecard
  distribution counts suppliers by that grade.
- **Audit currency** = `next_audit_due` − as-of in days; **overdue** = negative (as-of
  after the due date). Reported per supplier and as the overdue count.
- **Single-source exposure** = suppliers with `single_source == "true"`, listed with
  their window reject rate, grade, and audit status; the risk severity is computed
  (high when above ceiling or audit overdue).
- **History** = monthly reject rate across all periods for all suppliers plus each
  supplier above the ceiling (≤4 lines).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-05.1** — Every supplier on the approved list has a current quality-system audit
  _(basis: stand-in; the approved-supplier procedure on record does not state the audit
  interval used here [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule: met if no supplier's `next_audit_due` is
  before as-of.
- **E-05.2** — Incoming reject rate stays under the ceiling for every supplier _(basis:
  stand-in reject ceiling; no supplier-quality target on record [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule:
  met if every supplier's window reject rate ≤ `reject_ceiling_pct`.
- **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline names every supplier above the ceiling
  (with single-source and approval-status flags), every overdue audit with days overdue,
  the single-source families, and the scorecard distribution.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: window reject rate and on-time by supplier and family, the
scorecard distribution, which suppliers are single-source and their posture, and audit
currency vs the as-of date. It does **not** assert: why lots were rejected, the
production impact of a rejection (line stoppages are not in the pin), whether the
scorecard formula is right (it is the system of record's), or that the ceiling / audit
rule are correct (both expectations are unvalidated).
