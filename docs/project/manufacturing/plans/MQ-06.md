## Question

Component obsolescence risk by hardware revision — which parts on which revs are single-sourced, end-of-life, or on conditional suppliers?

**Asked by**: supplier-quality, vp-manufacturing · **cadence**: quarterly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The quarterly sourcing review: which components on which in-production hardware
revisions need a second source, a last-time buy, or a design change — before a supplier
notice turns into a line stop.

## Approach

Roadmap — **not implemented**. Committed definitions for when it is:

- **Exposure per (hw rev, component)** = single-source flag × supplier approval status
  × end-of-life notice, joined through the bill of materials.
- **Ranked list** = components ordered by exposure with the revisions and the monthly
  volume they carry (from the lot records).

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-suppliers`

Data we have: supplier register with single-source flag, approval status, family. Data
we still need (the gap that keeps this question `not-implemented`): a **bill of
materials by hardware revision** (which supplier part goes on which rev) and **supplier
end-of-life / product-change notices** — neither exists as a corpus dataset.
