## Question

First-pass yield, scrap and rework by product line, hw rev, station and site — where is yield eroding, and since when?

**Asked by**: vp-manufacturing, plant-manager · **cadence**: weekly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The weekly plant review: whether the VP of manufacturing opens a containment /
investigation on a specific line × station cell, whether the PP3500 rev C ramp is gated
on yield, and where engineering attention goes first. The answer must separate a healthy
portfolio average from one eroding cell underneath it, and it must date the onset so
the investigation starts at the right month — not at a recollection.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **Window** = the trailing `window_months` calendar months (catalog `params`, 3) ending
  with the month of the pinned snapshot's provenance `as_of`; the "prior window" is the
  same length immediately before it. Lots are assigned to a month by `start_date`.
- **First-pass yield (FPY)** = Σ `units_passed_first_time` ÷ Σ `units_started` over the
  cell, **unit-weighted** (not an average of lot yields). **Scrap rate** and **rework
  rate** use the same denominator; they sum to the first-pass failure rate.
- **Cuts**: product line; station; site; PP3500 by hw rev × station; and every product
  line × station cell.
- **Eroding cell** = a line × station cell whose window FPY is below `fpy_floor_pct`.
  **Onset ("since when")** = the first month of the unbroken run of months, ending at
  the as-of month, whose **rolling trailing-window FPY** (same window length, ending at
  that month) is below the floor — scanned backward, the run breaks at the first month
  at or above the floor. A cell that dipped and recovered is not reported as eroding.
- **Rev-specific gap** = for PP3500, the (rev, station) cell with the lowest window FPY,
  compared with the best other rev at the same station; its onset is computed the same
  way. The claim "rev-specific, not station drift" is computed from that comparison,
  never narrated.
- **History** = monthly FPY for the portfolio and for the widest-gap rev/station cell and
  its peers (≤4 lines); months before a population exists are omitted, not zero-filled
  (a rev not yet in production is absent, not zero-yield).
- The floor and every threshold comparison run on **unrounded** values; rounding is
  render-only.

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-01.1** — Every product line holds first-pass yield at or above the floor over the
  trailing window _(basis: stand-in yield floor; no per-line target is on record in a
  manufacturing plan or the pFMEA [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule: met if every line's window
  FPY ≥ floor, otherwise not-met. **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline carries
  the count of lines and of line × station cells below the floor plus the rev-specific
  gap and its onset month, so a badge-only rollup cannot hide one eroding cell behind a
  passing portfolio.
- No assumption records are cited; the answer computes from the pin alone.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: window FPY, scrap and rework by line / station / site / PP3500 rev,
which line × station cells sit below the (stand-in) floor, and the computed onset month
of each. It does **not** assert: the failure mode behind a dip (no defect codes in the
pin), whether reworked units eventually passed (not tracked), the cost of the yield
loss (MQ-09), or whether the floor itself is right (E-01.1 is unvalidated).
