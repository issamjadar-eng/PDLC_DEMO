# Analysis plan — MQ-03

_The analysis contract for this question: what the answer is FOR, how it is computed,
what it assumes, and what it does — and does not — assert. User-owned prose: edit
freely. Editions pin this file's hash; the quality audit calls out drift, and an agent

## Question

NCR rate and aging, CAPA open count, past-due share and effectiveness-verification backlog — by source and severity.

**Asked by**: vp-quality · **cadence**: weekly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The weekly quality-system review and the management-review input: whether the VP of
quality escalates past-due CAPAs (critical ones first), re-baselines the CAPA
investigation capacity, and prioritizes the effectiveness-verification backlog — and
whether the supplier nonconformance load points at one supplier (hand-off to MQ-05).
The answer exists so an auditor's first three findings (critical CAPA past due, closures
without effectiveness verification, aging NCRs) are known before the audit.

## Approach

Operational definitions this answer commits to:

- **As-of** = the pinned snapshot's provenance `as_of`. **Open** = `opened_date` ≤ as-of
  and no `closed_date` (or `closed_date` > as-of). **Past due** = open and `due_date` <
  as-of. The register's own `status` column is vocabulary-asserted but the populations
  are recomputed from dates, so the answer never depends on the export's status logic.
- **Aging** = days from `opened_date` to as-of for open NCRs (mean and median); **days
  over due** = as-of − `due_date` for past-due CAPAs (median).
- **Effectiveness-verified share** = CAPAs with `effectiveness_verified == "true"` ÷ all
  CAPAs with a `closed_date`; the **backlog** is the complement. Cut by closure quarter.
- **NCR intake** = NCRs opened per month; the headline compares the trailing three
  months' mean with the prior three months.
- **Past-due history** = at each month-end in the pinned range, the count of CAPAs open
  and past due at that date, reconstructed from opened / due / closed dates (exact for
  this register; needs no prior snapshots). "Rising" is computed as last month-end >
  the month-end six months earlier.
- **Supplier concentration** = supplier-sourced NCRs grouped by `supplier_id` over the
  whole register; the top supplier's share is reported and the ≥40% threshold for the
  concentration risk is a computed comparison.
- All threshold comparisons run on unrounded values; rounding is render-only.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-ncr-capa` (NCR + CAPA register: type, opened / closed / due
  dates, status, source, product line, severity, root-cause category, effectiveness
  verification, NCR→CAPA link, supplier id)

Data we have: everything above, 2025-01..2026-08, as-of 2026-08-31. Data we still need
(stated gaps, never faked): units produced per month (to express an NCR rate per unit —
roadmap MQ-04 via the lot dataset), CAPA cost (MQ-09), and the text of each
investigation (root-cause category is the only classification in the pin).

## Assumptions & expectations

- **E-03.1** — No critical CAPA is past its due date _(basis: stand-in; the CAPA
  procedure on record names no escalation rule for critical past-due items [VERIFY])_.
  Verdict rule: met if the count of open, past-due, severity-critical CAPAs at as-of is
  zero.
- **E-03.2** — Closed CAPAs carry a completed effectiveness verification _(basis:
  stand-in; no verification-completion target on record [VERIFY])_. Verdict rule: met if
  the verified share ≥ `effectiveness_verified_floor_pct` (catalog `params`, 90).
- **Rollup-survival rule**: the verdict headline carries the open and past-due CAPA
  counts, the critical past-due count, the verified share against its floor and the
  backlog count, the open-NCR count and mean age, and the top supplier's share — so a
  one-line rollup cannot hide a critical item behind a healthy total.

## Assertions & limits

This answer asserts: open / past-due / critical-past-due CAPA counts and their history,
the effectiveness-verified share and backlog by closure quarter, open-NCR counts and
ages by severity, NCR intake by source, and supplier concentration of supplier-sourced
NCRs. It does **not** assert: an NCR rate per unit (no production denominator pinned),
root cause beyond the register's category, whether a specific CAPA's fix is adequate,
or that the floors are correct (both expectations are unvalidated).
