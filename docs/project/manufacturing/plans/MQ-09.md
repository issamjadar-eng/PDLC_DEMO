## Question

Cost of scrap and rework by line and station — what is yield loss costing, and where does a yield point buy the most?

**Asked by**: coo, vp-manufacturing · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly cost-of-quality review: which yield-improvement project the COO funds first,
ranked by the dollars a yield point is worth at each line × station rather than by the
yield percentage alone.

## Approach

Roadmap — **not implemented**. Committed definitions for when it is:

- **Scrap cost** = units scrapped × standard cost accumulated through that station;
  **rework cost** = units reworked × standard rework cost per station.
- **Value of a yield point** = units started per month × 1% × the station's accumulated
  standard cost, per line × station.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-production-lots`
- `finance/internal-standard-costs`

Data we have: units scrapped / reworked / started per lot, line, station. Data we still
need (the gap that keeps this question `not-implemented`): **standard costs per line
and station** (material + labor + overhead accumulated through each step, and a rework
cost per station) from the finance domain — no such dataset is registered yet.
