## Question

Capacity vs the demand plan — can each line and site build the planned units, and where is the constraint?

**Asked by**: coo, vp-manufacturing · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The monthly S&OP call: whether the COO adds shifts, tooling, or a second line at a
site, or reshapes the demand plan — decided from demonstrated throughput per line ×
station against planned units, not from nameplate capacity.

## Approach

Roadmap — **not implemented**. Committed definitions for when it is:

- **Demonstrated throughput** = units started per station per month from the lot
  records (trailing three months), by line and site.
- **Planned units** = the demand plan's unit phasing per line per month.
- **Constraint** = the station whose demonstrated throughput ÷ planned units is lowest
  per line × site; reported with the shortfall in units and the months it binds.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-production-lots`
- `commercial/internal-revenue-plan`

Data we have: units started per lot by line / station / site / month. Data we still
need (the gap that keeps this question `not-implemented`): **planned units** — the
revenue plan of record carries revenue only, no unit phasing — and a station capacity
model (available hours × cycle time) to express headroom rather than only demonstrated
throughput.

## Assumptions & expectations

- (none declared yet) — an expectation on planned-units coverage will be declared when

## Assertions & limits

Once implemented the answer will assert demonstrated throughput vs planned units and the
binding station; it will not assert theoretical capacity or a staffing recommendation.
