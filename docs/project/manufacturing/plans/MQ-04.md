## Question

Lot release lead time and DHR completeness — where are lots queuing before release, and is it getting worse?

**Asked by**: plant-manager, vp-quality · **cadence**: weekly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The weekly release-board review: whether the plant manager adds DHR-review capacity,
fixes a documentation step, or escalates a site whose release lead time is drifting —
and how much finished inventory is sitting unreleased.

## Approach

Roadmap — **not implemented**. Committed definitions for when it is:

- **Release lead time** = `release_lead_days` for released lots (median and p90 per
  site and station, by start month); the trend is the monthly median per site.
- **Unreleased** = lots without a `release_date`, split into **in process** (DHR
  complete, started within the site's typical lead time) and **overdue for release**
  (older than the site's p90 lead time), and **blocked** (`dhr_complete == false`).
- **Getting worse** = a computed comparison of the trailing three months' median lead
  time against the prior three months, per site.

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-production-lots`

Data we have: start / release dates, lead days, DHR completeness per lot. Data we still
need (the gap that keeps this question `not-implemented`): the **release-step
timestamps** (test complete → DHR review → QA release) that locate *where* the queue is
— the lot record carries only start and release dates, so "where lots queue" cannot yet
be answered, only "how long".

## Assumptions & expectations

- (none declared yet) — a lead-time target per site will be declared once the plant's
  release SLA is on record.

## Assertions & limits

Once implemented the answer will assert lead-time distributions and unreleased inventory
by site; it will not attribute the queue to a specific step until step timestamps exist.
