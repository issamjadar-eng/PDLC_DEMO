## Question

Design-transfer readiness per line — are validations, DHR templates, work instructions and training complete for each revision in production?

**Asked by**: vp-manufacturing, vp-quality · **cadence**: monthly

## Goal — the decision this answer serves

The design-transfer review for each hardware revision entering or in production:
whether the VP of manufacturing signs the transfer as complete, or holds the revision's
release on a named open item — validation, DHR template, work instruction, or training.

## Approach

Roadmap — **not implemented**. Committed definitions for when it is:

- **Readiness per (line, hw rev)** = the checklist of transfer items (IQ/OQ/PQ of every
  rev-specific process and fixture, DHR template approved, work instructions released,
  operator training complete) with each item's status and due date.
- **Blocking item** = any checklist item not complete for a revision that has lots in
  production (from the lot records).

## Data

Registered corpus dependencies:
- `manufacturing/internal-process-validation`
- `manufacturing/internal-production-lots`

Data we have: validation status per asset / process / line; which revisions have lots
in production. Data we still need (the gap that keeps this question
`not-implemented`): a **design-transfer checklist register** — DHR template approvals,
work-instruction release status, and training records per revision — none of which is a
corpus dataset yet. MQ-08 answers the validation-only slice for PP3500 rev C in the
meantime.

Once implemented the answer will assert per-revision checklist completeness; it will not
substitute for the design-transfer sign-off itself.
