05Agentic Delivery§1.2 · Trust mechanism
The Load-Bearing Distinction
The model is stochastic at generation. The artifact is deterministic the moment a human approves and commits it.
A regulator is not approving the model. A regulator is approving the artifact.

Iterate-then-fix is how every regulated deliverable has always worked — code, DHF, V&V protocols, submissions, PSURs. Agentic generation does not change the discipline; it changes the input method.

06Agentic Delivery§1.2 · Trust · wave collapse
Variation A · The Wave Collapses at Commit

Stochastic at generation. Deterministic at commit.

The probabilistic step is an authoring step that happens once per change. By the time a regulator sees the artifact, there is no probability left in it. A regulator is not approving the model — a regulator is approving the artifact.

11Agentic Delivery§3.1 · Vocabulary
Three Terms, Three Disciplines

PDLC, SDLC, AI-in-Product — they are not the same.

PDLC — the container
Product Development Life Cycle
Discovery → design controls → V&V → submission → manufacturing → post-market. Every regulated artifact lives inside it.
SDLC — a slice of PDLC
Software Development Life Cycle
Software-specific lifecycle. Governed by IEC 62304 in HCLS. SRS, source, tests, releases.
AI-in-Product
AI inside what ships
The model executes at runtime as part of the clinical or operational function. Imaging triage, CDS, adaptive alarms.
This paper is about a fourth thing
Agentic engineering / AI-in-PDLC tooling — the team's working method to produce the regulated artifacts. It points at the artifact set from outside; it is not part of the product.
12Agentic Delivery§3.1 · Vocabulary · nested view
Variation A · Nested Containers

SDLC sits inside PDLC. AI-in-Product is a runtime resident.

Containment, not equivalence. Most "AI in our delivery" pitches treat these three as the same thing — they aren't. The team's tooling (the dashed arrow from outside) is what this paper is about.

13Agentic Delivery§3.1 · Vocabulary · lifecycle ring
Variation B · Lifecycle Ring

PDLC is strategic. SDLC is tactical delivery.

The takeaway
PDLC expands to strategic. SDLC is the tactical delivery slice.
  • PDLC = strategic envelope. 9 phases · adoption feeds next-gen discovery.
  • SDLC = subset slice. Amber arc covers Design Inputs → V&V (IEC 62304).
  • AI-in-Product rides inside PDLC at runtime.
  • AI-in-PDLC tooling points in from outside — the team's working method.

17Agentic Delivery§4.2 · Spec primacy
The Investment Ranking

Specs first. Scaffolding second. Model third.

01 Specs
Largest value delta
Every repeatable quality improvement in the corpus was a spec improvement. Every recurring complaint becomes a versioned spec change before any model-tuning is attempted.
02 Scaffolding
Medium value delta · infrastructure
Skills · hooks · registries · idempotency contracts. The infrastructure that makes specs operationally meaningful and propagatable across customer programs.
03 Model choice
Tuning lever only
Real, but smallest. Bigger model for harder synthesis; faster model for high-volume routine work.
The invariant
A score performs the same way under any competent orchestra. A spec produces the same artifact under any competent model. The score travels.
18Agentic Delivery§4.4 · Guardrails
The Defense-in-Depth

Six layers of guardrails. Not one.

In an unregulated context, a single L1 gate is often enough. In HCLS, any single layer's failure means the entire compliance posture collapses. Multi-model substrate is part of the layered defense — adversarial validation requires different models checking each other.

L1
Pre-action gates
Block before the action. Task gate · freeze gate.
L2
Authoring discipline
CLAUDE.md rules · [VERIFY] · default-to-action.
L3
In-flight verification
Advisor counterpoint · multi-perspective panels.
L4
Post-action checks
Best-practices audit · regression-guard lint.
L5
Release gates
Named-reviewer attestation · Comala (Part 11).
L6
Continuous improvement
Lessons harvest · digest · sync-log.

Where the Discipline Shows Up

Two real redirects (anonymized).

Example B · Domain Mode Severity · High
Reshaping the regulatory architecture.
Model
behavior
Scaffolded a unified single-DHF for a digital-surgery program shipping three software modules — filing-aligned, superficially clean.
Lead's
redirect
Engineering reality is three modules · three cadences · three teams · three IEC 62304 classifications. Maps cleanly to § 5's system/item hierarchy.
Codified
rule
Multi-module programs require flat system + items multi-DHF. Anchored against IEC 62304 § 5, FDA multi-function device guidance, 510(k) precedent.
If missed
Single-DHF filed → 510(k) submitted on the wrong architecture → regulatory rework after FDA AI requests, schema rebuild, four skills rewritten, CLAUDE.md DHF section replaced. Architectural restart, not a fix.

The agentic project shape is not an authored design — it is the codified residue of a human running an eval loop in real time, paired with a human bringing outside regulatory and domain knowledge. The artifacts are the receipts.

23Agentic Delivery§5.2 · Domain Mode (deep dive)
Variation · Single-Redirect Deep Dive

Example B — Domain Mode.

Severity · High · regulatory architecture

Reshaping the regulatory architecture.

Model
behavior
Scaffolded the project's design history file as a unified single-DHF — filing-aligned, superficially clean, internally consistent.
Lead's
redirect
"Engineering reality is three modules with three cadences, three teams, three IEC 62304 classifications. Maps cleanly to § 5's software-system / software-item hierarchy."
Why it
mattered
The constraint was not in the codebase at all. It came from outside: IEC 62304 § 5, FDA multi-function device guidance, and 510(k) precedent. None of which the model could have inferred from training. This is what domain expertise looks like.
Codified
as
Schema rewrite (role + composes fields in project.yml) · four skills rewritten for system/item roles · CLAUDE.md DHF section replaced with IEC 62304 § 5 cited inline.
If missed
Single-DHF filed → 510(k) submitted on the wrong architecture → FDA AI request requires architectural re-baseline → schema rebuild · four skills rewritten · CLAUDE.md replaced — but weeks into a submission cycle, not weeks into the design phase. Architectural restart, not a fix. Multi-week submission delay is the floor; worst case is a re-submission cycle.
