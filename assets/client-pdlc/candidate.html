The model is stochastic at generation. The artifact is deterministic the moment a human approves and commits it.
A regulator is not approving the model. A regulator is approving the artifact.

Hooks — automatic tripwires you can't forget to run

A hook is a small automation that fires at a specific moment — when a session starts, when a file is about to be edited, when work is about to end — and either allows, blocks, or adds context to the action.
