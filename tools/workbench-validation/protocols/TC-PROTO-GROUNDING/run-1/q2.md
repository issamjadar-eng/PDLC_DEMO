**Question (verbatim):** What does our regulatory strategy say about the PCCP scope for the AI/ML component?

**Answer (verbatim capture; absolute repository paths normalized to repo-relative):**

## Short answer
The regulatory strategy does **not** put an AI/ML component in the cleared PP3500 baseline. It reserves a **slot** for one inside the PCA device's PCCP — the "predictive-alarm SaMD pathway" — and leaves open whether that slot is used or AI/ML lands via a **future filing**. PCCP scope as a whole is still a pending decision. The DHF PCCP document is an empty stub.
