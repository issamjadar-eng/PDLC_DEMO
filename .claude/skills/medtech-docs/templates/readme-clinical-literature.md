# Clinical Literature

Published studies, clinical evidence, and surgical/clinical workflow references that support the regulatory filing.

## Expected Content

- Published clinical studies on the device's clinical domain and outcomes
- Surgical/clinical workflow documentation and technique references
- Safety and efficacy data for predicate or comparable devices
- Clinical evidence supporting intended use and indications for use

## Conventions

- **Naming**: `author-year-short-topic.md` for summaries; original PDFs in a `source/` subfolder if needed
- Note publication date, journal/source, and relevance to the filing
- Flag studies with limitations or conflicts of interest
- Link to the task that initiated the literature review
