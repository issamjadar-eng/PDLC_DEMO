## Scope

This guidance describes the mechanisms by which submitters can request interactions with FDA related to medical device submissions — written feedback and/or meetings on planned or submitted IDEs, PMAs, HDEs, De Novo requests, 510(k)s, CW (CLIA Waiver), Duals (510(k) + CW), Accessory Classification Requests, BLAs, and certain INDs for biological-product-regulated devices reviewed by CBER.

The guidance defines **five Q-Submission types covered in detail** (Sections II.A–E), plus **other interactions tracked with a "Q" number** under separate guidance (Section II.F: Breakthrough, STeP, Accessory Classification), other Q-Sub program uses (Section II.G), and interactions explicitly NOT within Q-Sub scope (Section II.H, e.g., TAP Pilot).

## Five Q-Submission Types (Sections II.A–E)

| Q-Sub Type | Purpose | Feedback Format | Section |
|------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| **Pre-Submission (Pre-Sub)** | Feedback on a planned IDE / PMA / HDE / De Novo / 510(k) / CW / Dual / Accessory Classification Request / BLA / IND, prior to submission. Voluntary; entirely submitter-initiated. | Written feedback; may include a meeting at the submitter's election | II.A |
| **Submission Issue Request (SIR)** | Discuss a proposed approach to address issues in a hold/clinical-hold letter (510(k)/De Novo/Dual AI letter; PMA/HDE Major-Deficiency/Approvable letters; BLA Complete Response). NOT a pre-review of the response. | Written feedback OR meeting (submitter specifies) | II.B |
| **Study Risk Determination** | Obtain FDA determination of significant-risk (SR) / nonsignificant-risk (NSR) / IDE-exempt status for a planned device clinical investigation per 21 CFR Part 812. FDA is the final arbiter. | Written determination | II.C |
| **Informational Meeting** | Share information with FDA *without* expectation of feedback. Useful for overview of ongoing development (especially multiple submissions in 6-12 months) or familiarizing a review team with new technology. | Listening session; clarifying questions allowed | II.D |
| **PMA Day 100 Meeting** | FDA's statutory obligation (§ 515(d)(3)(A)(i)) to meet with applicant no later than 100 days after a filed original PMA. Discuss review status, deficiencies, action plans. | In-person or virtual meeting | II.E |

**Pre-Subs and PCCPs.** Pre-Subs are highly recommended for obtaining FDA feedback on Predetermined Change Control Plans (PCCPs) per FD&C Act § 515C (added by FDORA § 3308, 2022). PCCP-relevant Pre-Sub topics include scope/appropriateness, change categories, modification protocols and validation, performance acceptance criteria, and post-market monitoring. PCCP authorization replaces the supplemental-PMA / new-510(k) gate for in-scope changes.

## Other Q-Submission Types (Section II.F — tracked under separate guidance)

| Q-Sub Type | Governing Guidance |
|---|---|
| Agreement and Determination Meetings | FDAMA Early Collaboration Meetings guidance |
| Breakthrough Device Designation Request + Breakthrough Interaction | Breakthrough Devices Program guidance (§ 515B) |
| STeP Entrance Request + STeP Interaction Submission | Safer Technologies Program for Medical Devices |
| Accessory Classification Request (existing or new accessory type) | Medical Device Accessories — Describing Accessories and Classification Pathways guidance (§ 513(f)(6)) |

These are tracked with a "Q" number but follow the processes in their own guidance documents. Pre-Subs may be used to discuss any of them before formal request.

## Other Uses of the Q-Sub Program (Section II.G)

Informational Meeting Q-Subs are used as a tracking vehicle for interactions that don't fit a defined Q-Sub type — cross-cutting policy questions, recognition of genetic-variant databases, non-clinical testing strategies from third-party labs, NSR/IDE-exempt study design feedback, device-led Combination Product Agreement Meetings (CPAM, § 503(g)(2)(A)), and certain device-quality/QMS feedback during product development.

## Interactions NOT Within the Q-Sub Program (Section II.H)

Out-of-scope interactions explicitly identified in Section II.H include the **TAP (Total product life-cycle Advisory Program) Pilot** — TAP interactions follow their own process and are not counted as Pre-Subs for MDUFA reporting.

**Required elements** (per Appendix 1 Pre-Sub Acceptance Checklist):

- **Cover letter** — stating this is a Pre-Sub; specifying meeting OR written-feedback-only; specifying meeting format (in-person or virtual)
- **Device description** — clear description of device, components, function, technology
- **Intended use / Indications for use** — proposed statement
- **Proposed predicate device(s)** — if applicable, with comparison
- **Specific questions for FDA** — focused, well-defined; **recommend ≤ 3-4 substantial topics maximum** (Q-Sub feedback most useful when questions are sharp; Appendix 2 provides example questions across review topics)
- **Supporting information** — sufficient context for FDA to understand and respond
- **Proposed regulatory pathway** — intended submission type (510(k), De Novo, PMA, HDE, IDE, etc.)
- **Administrative information** — contact, device name, product code (if known)

**Recommended but situational:**

- Proposed testing/study protocols
- Proposed clinical study design
- Preliminary performance data
- Risk analysis summary
- Comparison table (device vs. predicate)
- Literature references

**Submission format** (as of May 2025):

- **eCopy** is the current submission format to FDA Document Control Center per section 745A(b) of the FD&C Act and the eCopy Program guidance.
- **eSTAR for Q-Subs** is being introduced — see the draft guidance `qsub-estar-draft-distilled.md` (May 29, 2025 draft) for the future state. eSTAR templates are guided submission-preparation tools intended to improve consistency and review efficiency. Section 745A(b) of FD&C Act mandates electronic-format standards for submissions.

## MDUFA-V FDA Response Timelines (Pre-Subs only)

Pre-Subs are the only Q-Sub type with MDUFA performance commitments. No user fees apply to Q-Subs.

| Milestone | Timeline |
|-----------|----------|
| Acceptance Review (RTA) | Within 15 days of receipt (calendar days — per guidance footnote, "days refers to calendar days") |
| Meeting date determined | By Day 30 (per MDUFA IV/V refinement) |
| Written feedback provided (meeting request) | By Day 70 OR 5 days before meeting, whichever is sooner |
| Written feedback (no meeting) | By Day 70 |
| Meeting held | Typically Day 70–75 |
| Submitter drafts meeting minutes | Within 15 calendar days of meeting |
| FDA reviews meeting minutes | Within 30 days of receipt |

**SIR Timelines (two-tier system):**

- SIR submitted within 60 days of FDA's hold letter: feedback within **21 days**
- SIR submitted more than 60 days after FDA's hold letter: feedback within **70 days**

## Meeting Procedures

**Meeting formats:**
- Virtual (videoconference or teleconference) — most common, easier to schedule
- Face-to-face (in-person) — when demos or detailed review needed
- Written feedback only — if questions are straightforward

**Meeting details:**
- Typically 60 minutes
- FDA written feedback is provided before the meeting (review beforehand to focus discussion)
- **Submitter is responsible for drafting meeting minutes** within 15 calendar days
- FDA reviews submitter minutes within 30 days
- Requesting a meeting upfront is recommended — easier to cancel than to add later

## Multiple Pre-Subs for the Same Device

- Allowed, but avoid multiple open simultaneously for the same device/indication
- Sequential Pre-Subs on different topics are tracked as supplements in the same Q-Sub family
- If topics are close together in timing, combine into one Pre-Sub (up to 3–4 topics)
- Different indications for the same device may justify separate concurrent Pre-Subs

## FDA Feedback Stability

FDA intends that Pre-Sub feedback will not change at later review, provided:
1. Information in the future marketing submission is consistent with the Pre-Sub.
2. Data or other information does not raise important new issues materially affecting safety or effectiveness.

Issues raised in a Pre-Sub do not obligate submitters to resolve them in the subsequent submission — but any future submission related to the topic should discuss why a different approach was chosen or an issue left unresolved.

## Practical Planning Timeline

```
Day 0:     Pre-Sub submitted
Day 15:    RTA acceptance (or refuse — requires resubmission)
Day 30:    Meeting date scheduled (if meeting requested)
Day 65-70: Written feedback received
Day 70-75: Meeting held (if requested)
Day 75-90: Meeting minutes submitted by sponsor (within 15 days of the meeting)
Day 90-105: FDA reviews/finalizes minutes
Day 105+:   Incorporate feedback into submission drafting
```

**Planning estimate (not from the guidance): allow ~4–5 months** of lead time before a planned marketing submission to cover the full Pre-Sub cycle plus feedback-incorporation time.

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Q-Submission** | Any formal request for feedback from FDA on a medical device, tracked through the Q-Sub system with a "Q" number |
| **Pre-Submission (Pre-Sub)** | Q-Sub type for obtaining feedback on a planned premarket submission, IDE, IND, CW, Accessory Classification Request |
| **Submission Issue Request (SIR)** | Q-Sub type for discussing approaches to issues in FDA hold letters |
| **Study Risk Determination** | Q-Sub type for FDA SR/NSR/exempt determination on a planned clinical study |
| **Informational Meeting** | Q-Sub type for sharing information with FDA without expectation of feedback |
| **PMA Day 100 Meeting** | Statutory PMA-applicant meeting within 100 days of filing per FD&C Act § 515(d)(3)(A)(i) |
| **RTA (Refuse to Accept)** | FDA's initial determination of whether a Pre-Sub meets minimum content requirements (within 15 calendar days) |
| **Q-Sub Family** | Related Q-Subs for the same device/indication, tracked together with supplements and amendments |
| **CPAM** | Combination Product Agreement Meeting per § 503(g)(2)(A) — tracked as Informational Meeting Q-Sub |
