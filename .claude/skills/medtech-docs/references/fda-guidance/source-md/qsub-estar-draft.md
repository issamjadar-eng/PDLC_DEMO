13            Introduction
14   The Food and Drug Administration (FDA or Agency) is issuing this draft guidance document to
15   introduce submitters of Q-Submissions (Q-Subs) to the Center for Devices and Radiological
16   Health (CDRH) and Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) to the current
17   resources and associated content developed and made publicly available to support certain Q-Sub
18   electronic submissions to FDA, specifically Pre-Submissions (Pre-Subs). 1 This draft guidance is
19   intended to represent one of several steps in meeting FDA’s commitment to the development of
20   electronic submission templates to serve as guided submission preparation tools for industry to
21   improve submission consistency and enhance efficiency in the review process. 2 When finalized,
22   this guidance will also facilitate the implementation of the FDA’s mandate under section
23   745A(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act), amended by section 207 of
24   the FDA Reauthorization Act of 2017 (FDARA) (Pub. L. 115-52) to provide further standards
25   for the submission by electronic format, a timetable for establishment of these further standards,

 74   Section 745A(b) of the FD&C Act, amended by section 207 of FDARA, requires that pre-
 75   submissions and submissions for devices under sections 510(k), 513(f)(2)(A), 515(c), 515(d),
 76   515(f), 520(g), 520(m), or 564 of the FD&C Act or section 351 of the Public Health Service Act,

 78   submissions, be submitted in electronic format specified by FDA beginning on such date as
 79   specified by FDA in final guidance. It also mandates that FDA issue draft guidance not later than
 80   October 1, 2019, and a final guidance not later than 1 year after the close of the public comment
 81   period, providing for further standards for the submission by electronic format, a timetable for
 82   establishment of these further standards, and criteria for waivers of and exemptions from the

 88   tools for industry to improve submission consistency and enhance efficiency in the review

 91   states that “[n]o later than 12 months after the close of the public comment period, the Agency
 92   will issue a final guidance.” The 745A(b) device parent guidance was intended to satisfy the final
 93   guidance documents referenced in section 745A(b)(3) of the FD&C Act and the MDUFA IV

 95   Commitment Letter affirmed FDA’s commitment to the continued development of electronic
 96   submission templates for a variety of premarket submission types. 5

108           Scope
109   This guidance describes the technical standards associated with preparation of the electronic
110   submission template for Q-Subs that enable submission of the electronic Q-Sub solely in
111   electronic format. Currently, Pre-Subs are the only type of Q-Sub available for electronic
112   submission. This guidance document will be updated accordingly as other Q-Sub types are
113   implemented within the eSTAR program. The electronic submission template includes the
114   information and guided prompts FDA believes will best facilitate the collection and assembly of
115   the necessary elements of a ‘complete’ submission. 8 This guidance is not intended to specify the
116   user-interface and detailed content of the eSTAR, but instead is limited to establishing the Q-Sub
117   electronic format and standards for complying with section 745(A)(b)(3) of the FD&C Act. FDA
118   intends to implement new versions of eSTAR as relevant policies change. FDA also has an
119   ongoing process to collect and consider public comments and stakeholder feedback, which is
120   described on FDA’s website. 9

153       Current Electronic Submission Template Structure,
154   Format, and Use
155   The electronic submission template, eSTAR, is the only currently available electronic
156   submission template at this time to facilitate the preparation of Q-Sub electronic submissions.
157   eSTAR consists of a collection of questions, text, logic, and prompts within a template that
158   guides a user through construction of a ‘complete’ Q-Sub 15 submission. eSTAR is highly
159   automated, includes integrated databases (e.g., FDA product codes, FDA-recognized voluntary
160   consensus standards), and includes targeted questions designed to collect specific data and
161   information from the submitter. eSTAR also includes applicable links to regulations, relevant
162   guidances, and other resources for the submitter’s reference. Finally, eSTAR is structured to
163   collect and assemble content in the Q-Sub as an electronic submission that closely follows the
164   content of the “SMART” Q-Sub review memo template, an internal review tool used by FDA
165   reviewers.
166
167   Given that an electronic submission properly prepared with an electronic submission template
168   should represent a complete submission, 16 eSTAR submissions are not anticipated to undergo a
169   refuse to accept (RTA) process. However, FDA intends to employ a virus scanning and technical
170   screening process for an eSTAR. A technical screening process is a process for verifying that
171   eSTAR responses accurately describe the device(s) (e.g., there are, in fact, no tissue contacting
172   components if indicated as such) and that there is at least one relevant attachment per each
173   applicable attachment-type question (e.g., a Product Description attachment is included in
174   response to the Product Description question). 17 For Pre-Subs, the technical screening process is
