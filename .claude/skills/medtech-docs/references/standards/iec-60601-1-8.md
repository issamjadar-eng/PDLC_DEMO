## Overview

IEC 60601-1-8 is the collateral standard in the IEC 60601 family that specifies basic safety and essential performance requirements and tests for **alarm systems** in medical electrical equipment and medical electrical systems, and provides guidance for their application. Per the publisher's abstract, it "defines alarm categories (priorities) by degree of urgency, consistent alarm signals and consistent control states and their marking for all alarm systems" ([IEC webstore abstract](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67388)).

As a collateral standard it applies **in conjunction with** the general standard IEC 60601-1 and any applicable particular standard (for example, an infusion-pump particular standard may modify or add alarm requirements for that device type — consult the particular standard's alarm clauses alongside this one). ## Alarm Priority Concept

The publicly verifiable core concept (per the IEC abstract) is that the standard defines **alarm categories (priorities) by degree of urgency**. The conventional priority scheme is:

- **High priority** — [VERIFY] immediate operator response required
- **Medium priority** — [VERIFY] prompt operator response required
- **Low priority** — [VERIFY] operator awareness required
